# Table Reference

The rules you reach for mid-encounter — each entry links to the full rule.
Print this page for a one-sheet GM screen. *(One-liners are paraphrases —
click through for the exact rule text.)*

## Your Turn

On your turn you get **one main action, one [maneuver](Browse/feature/common/maneuvers/index.md),
and one [move action](Browse/movement/index.md)** — in any order, and you can
trade your main action for an extra maneuver or move action. Free maneuvers
are unlimited. [Triggered actions](Browse/rule/combat/triggered-action.md)
happen off-turn — one per round, though
[free triggered actions](Browse/rule/combat/triggered-action.md) are unlimited. Your
[heroic resource](Browse/rule/resource/heroic-resource.md) builds every turn — spend it.

## The Power Roll

**2d10 + characteristic** → three [tiers](Browse/rule/dice/tier-outcome.md):

| Roll | Tier |
|---|---|
| ≤ 11 | Tier 1 (weakest) |
| 12–16 | Tier 2 |
| 17 + | Tier 3 (strongest) |

- An [edge](Browse/rule/dice/edge.md) adds +2; a [bane](Browse/rule/dice/bane.md) −2.
- A **double edge / double bane** moves the result one tier up/down instead.
- Mixing them: an edge and a bane (or double edge and double bane) cancel out;
  a double edge with one bane leaves an edge; a double bane with one edge
  leaves a bane.
- A [natural 19–20](Browse/rule/dice/natural-19-20.md) is a critical: automatic
  tier 3 and you can take an extra main action.
- Tests use the same roll — see [test difficulty](Browse/rule/test/test-difficulty.md).
  Having an applicable [skill](Browse/skill/index.md) gives a
  [test](Browse/rule/test/test.md) (not an ability roll) a +2.

## Hero Tokens & Surges

[Hero tokens](Browse/rule/resource/hero-token.md) are a shared pool — one per
hero at session start, gone at session end. One token benefit per turn (or per
test):

- **1 token** → gain 2 surges.
- **1 token** → turn a failed saving throw into a success.
- **1 token** → reroll a test (keep the new roll).
- **2 tokens** → on your turn or when damaged (no action), regain Stamina
  equal to your Recovery value — applied before the damage.

[Surges](Browse/rule/resource/surge.md): when you deal rolled damage, spend up
to 3 — each adds your **highest characteristic** in damage to one target; or
spend 2 to raise one of the ability's
[potencies](Browse/rule/character/potency.md) by 1 for one target (never more
than +1 each, but you can pay again for other targets). Unspent surges vanish
when combat ends.

## Conditions

| Condition | In one line |
|---|---|
| [Bleeding](Browse/condition/bleeding.md) | Lose 1d6 + level Stamina after each main action, triggered action, or Might/Agility roll. |
| [Dazed](Browse/condition/dazed.md) | One thing per turn (main action *or* maneuver *or* move); no triggered actions. |
| [Frightened](Browse/condition/frightened.md) | Bane vs the source; the source has edge vs you; can't willingly move closer to it. |
| [Grabbed](Browse/condition/grabbed.md) | Speed 0; only the grabber can force-move you; bane on abilities that don't target the grabber. |
| [Prone](Browse/condition/prone.md) | Your strikes take a bane; melee vs you has edge; crawl (+1 square per square). |
| [Restrained](Browse/condition/restrained.md) | Speed 0, no Stand Up, can't be force-moved; bane on ability rolls and M/A tests; abilities vs you have edge. |
| [Slowed](Browse/condition/slowed.md) | Speed 2; can't shift. |
| [Taunted](Browse/condition/taunted.md) | Double bane on abilities that don't target the taunter (while you have line of effect to them). |
| [Weakened](Browse/condition/weakened.md) | Bane on power rolls. |

**Ending effects:** *(save ends)* → roll a d10 at the end of your turn, 6+
clears it ([saving throw](Browse/rule/general/saving-throw.md)); *(EoT)* →
ends at the [end of the affected creature's next turn](Browse/rule/combat/end-of-turn.md).

**Potency notation:** "M &lt; 2" means a target whose
[Might](Browse/rule/character/might.md) is 2 or higher shrugs off the effect —
the letter is the resisting characteristic, the number is your
[potency](Browse/rule/character/potency.md).

## Movement

- Every adjacent square costs 1 — diagonals too, but you can't cut a corner
  past a wall.
- A move never exceeds your speed unless an effect says so.
- [Difficult terrain](Browse/movement/difficult-terrain.md) +1 square per
  square · [climb / swim](Browse/movement/climb-or-swim.md) (2 per square
  without a climb/swim speed) · [crawl](Browse/movement/crawl.md) (+1 per
  square, while prone) · [fly](Browse/movement/fly.md) ·
  [falling](Browse/rule/health/falling.md)
- [Jump](Browse/movement/jump.md): long-jump up to Might *or* Agility squares
  (min 1), 1 square high, free during any move; a Might/Agility test stretches
  it (12–16: +1, 17+: +2 length and height). No jumping out of difficult or
  damaging terrain.
- [Shifting](Browse/movement/shifting.md): no opportunity attacks; can't shift
  into difficult terrain.
- [Teleport](Browse/movement/teleport.md): ignores obstacles and opportunity
  attacks; needs line of effect to an unoccupied destination; ends grabbed /
  restrained; you can stand on arrival.
- [Size](Browse/rule/character/size.md): squares occupied; size-1 creatures
  are 1T &lt; 1S &lt; 1M &lt; 1L, all in one square.

### Forced Movement

[Full rule →](Browse/movement/forced-movement.md)

- **Push** = away from you · **pull** = toward you · **slide** = any direction;
  none move the target vertically unless marked **vertical**. You may always
  move the target fewer squares.
- [Stability](Browse/rule/character/stability.md) subtracts its value from
  every forced move against you.
- Bigger attacker, smaller target (melee weapon ability): distance +1; the
  reverse changes nothing.
- **Slam into a creature**: movement stops, both take 1 damage per unspent
  square. **Into an object** (their size+): movement stops, target takes 2 +
  1 per unspent square.
- **Through objects** — squares of forced movement spent / damage taken:
  glass 1 / 3 · wood 3 / 5 · stone 6 / 8 · metal 9 / 11; leftover movement
  keeps going.

## Stamina, Dying & Recovery

- Damage reduces [Stamina](Browse/rule/health/stamina.md); when a non-hero
  hits 0, *you* choose death or unconsciousness.
- [Damage immunity](Browse/rule/damage/damage-immunity.md) subtracts its value
  from that damage type (to 0 = no rider effects);
  [weakness](Browse/rule/damage/damage-weakness.md) adds its value.
- [Winded](Browse/rule/health/winded.md): at or below half Stamina.
- [Dying](Browse/rule/health/dying.md): Stamina 0 or below — you keep acting,
  but you're bleeding and can't Catch Breath; you die at the negative of your
  winded value.
- Spend a [Recovery](Browse/rule/health/recoveries.md) to regain your recovery
  value ([Catch Breath](Browse/feature/common/maneuvers/catch-breath.md)
  maneuver); a [respite](Browse/rule/resource/respite.md) (24h safe rest)
  restores everything and converts Victories to XP.

## Combat Modifiers

- [Flanking](Browse/rule/combat/flanking.md): you + ally adjacent on opposite
  sides → edge on your melee strikes.
- [Cover](Browse/rule/combat/cover.md): half the target behind something solid
  → bane on damage-dealing abilities against them.
- [Concealment](Browse/rule/combat/concealment.md): obscured but not blocked
  (darkness, fog, invisibility) → strikes take a bane; a hidden target can't
  be targeted at all.
- [High ground](Browse/movement/high-ground.md): fully above the target →
  edge on the power roll.
- [Line of effect](Browse/rule/combat/line-of-effect.md): fully blocked by
  solid obstruction = no targeting.
- [Opportunity attacks](Browse/rule/combat/opportunity-attack.md): leaving a
  creature's reach without shifting → they get a melee free strike (free
  triggered action).
- [Surprised](Browse/rule/combat/surprised.md)

  ([encounter building](Read/bestiary/monster-basics.md)).

  budget cost; [minions](Browse/rule/organization/minion.md) are bought 4 at a time.
