# Shifting

---

[Shifting](shifting.md) is a careful form of movement that allows a creature to move safely past dangerous foes. Certain abilities, features, and other rules allow you to [shift](shifting.md) a specific number of squares, sometimes up to your [speed](../rule/character/speed.md). Whenever you [shift](shifting.md), creatures can't make [opportunity attacks](../rule/combat/opportunity-attack.md) against you triggered by that movement (see [Opportunity Attacks](../rule/combat/opportunity-attack.md) later in this chapter).

You can't [shift](shifting.md) into or while within [difficult terrain](difficult-terrain.md) or [damaging terrain](damaging-terrain.md) (see below). If a rule allows you to [shift](shifting.md), you can choose to instead move up to the number of squares you would have shifted (for example, to get out of [difficult terrain](difficult-terrain.md)). However, you can't combine moving and [shifting](shifting.md) within that movement.
