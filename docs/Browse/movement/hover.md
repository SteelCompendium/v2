# Hover

---

A creature who has "[hover](hover.md)" in their [speed](../rule/character/speed.md) entry (most commonly alongside "[fly](fly.md)" or "[teleport](teleport.md)"), or who gains the temporary ability to [hover](hover.md), can remain motionless in midair. They don't fall even if they are [knocked prone](../condition/prone.md) or their [speed](../rule/character/speed.md) is reduced to 0.
