# Damaging Terrain

---

Areas of acid, fire, sharp rocks, lava, or any other terrain that causes damage to creatures within it is [damaging terrain](damaging-terrain.md). The damage dealt by [damaging terrain](damaging-terrain.md) is noted in the terrain's description or in the description of the effect that creates the terrain.
