# Teleport

---

When a creature [teleports](teleport.md), they move from one space to another space instantaneously. The following rules apply to [teleporting](teleport.md):

- [Teleporting](teleport.md) doesn't provoke opportunity attacks or other effects that are triggered by a creature moving.
- When a creature [teleports](teleport.md), they bypass any obstacles between the space they leave and their destination space.
- A creature [teleporting](teleport.md) themself must have line of effect to their destination space. A creature [teleporting](teleport.md) another creature must have line of effect from the space the [teleport](teleport.md)ed creature leaves and to their destination space.
- A [teleporting](teleport.md) creature's destination space can't be occupied by another creature or object.
- The effect that lets a creature [teleport](teleport.md) indicates how far they can [teleport](teleport.md). That distance can be greater than the creature's speed.
- If a creature can [teleport](teleport.md) as part of their usual movement, they can use the [Advance](../feature/common/move-actions/advance.md) move action to [teleport](teleport.md) a number of squares up to their usual speed, unmodified by conditions or effects.
- If a creature [teleports](teleport.md) [while prone](../condition/prone.md), they can be standing when they reach their destination space provided they are able to stand. If a prone creature is [teleport](teleport.md)ed by another creature, it is up to that creature whether the [teleport](teleport.md)ed creature remains prone or stands if they are able.
- If you [teleport](teleport.md) while affected by the [grabbed](../condition/grabbed.md) or [restrained](../condition/restrained.md) conditions, those conditions end for you.
- When a creature [teleports](teleport.md), they must leave the space where they start and enter a new space. A creature can't [teleport](teleport.md) to and from the same space.
