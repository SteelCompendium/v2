# Crawl

---

If you [are prone](../condition/prone.md) (see [Conditions](../rule/combat/condition.md) in Chapter 5: [Classes](../../Read/heroes/classes.md)), you can remain prone and [crawl](crawl.md) on the ground. Doing so costs you 1 additional square of movement for every square you [crawl](crawl.md). If you intentionally want to [crawl](crawl.md), you can [fall prone](../condition/prone.md) as a [free maneuver](../rule/combat/free-maneuver.md). While voluntarily prone, you can choose to stand as a [free maneuver](../rule/combat/free-maneuver.md).
