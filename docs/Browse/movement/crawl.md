# Crawl

---

If you [are prone](../condition/prone.md) (see Conditions in Chapter 5: [Classes](../../Read/heroes/classes.md)), you can remain prone and [crawl](crawl.md) on the ground. Doing so costs you 1 additional square of movement for every square you [crawl](crawl.md). If you intentionally want to [crawl](crawl.md), you can [fall prone](../condition/prone.md) as a free maneuver. While voluntarily prone, you can choose to stand as a free maneuver.
