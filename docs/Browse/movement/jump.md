# Jump

---

Whenever an effect allows you to move (including using the [Advance](../feature/common/move-actions/advance.md) move action), you can automatically long jump a number of squares up to your [Might](../rule/character/might.md) or [Agility](../rule/character/agility.md) score (your choice; minimum 1 square) as part of that movement. The height of your jump is automatically 1 square as part of that movement.

If you want to jump even longer or higher than your baseline jump allows, make a [Might](../rule/character/might.md) or [Agility](../rule/character/agility.md) [test](../rule/test/test.md):

**[Power Roll](../rule/dice/power-roll.md) + [Might](../rule/character/might.md) or [Agility](../rule/character/agility.md):**

- **≤11:** You don't jump any farther than your baseline jump allows.
- **12-16:** You jump 1 square longer and higher than your baseline jump allows.
- **17+:** You jump 2 squares longer and higher than your baseline jump allows.

You can't jump farther or higher than the [distance](../rule/combat/distance.md) of the effect that allows you to move. You can't jump out of [difficult terrain](difficult-terrain.md) or [damaging terrain](damaging-terrain.md) (see below).
