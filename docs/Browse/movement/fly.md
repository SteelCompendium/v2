# Fly

---

A creature who has "[fly](fly.md)" in their speed entry, or who gains the temporary ability to [fly](fly.md), can move through the air vertically or horizontally at full speed and remain in midair. If a [flying](fly.md) creature is [knocked prone](../condition/prone.md) or has their speed reduced to 0, they fall (see Falling below).
