# Summoner Successor

---

*When their leader fell, they started listening to me for some reason.*

**Prerequisite:** You can't use the [Call Forth](../feature/ability/summoner/level-1/call-forth.md) ability, and you defeat a leader or solo creature with a Summon or Call Forth ability, such as a [high elf](../ancestry/high-elf.md) ordinator.

**Effect:** Choose a signature minion from a [summoner's](../class/summoner.md) [portfolio](../feature/summoner/level-1/portfolio.md) that shares a keyword with one of the creatures the summoner could summon. You can summon three of that minion into a single squad (up to a maximum of four creatures) at the start of each of your [turns](../rule/combat/turn.md) in combat. The [distance](../rule/combat/distance.md) you can command them is equal to your ranged [free strike](../feature/common/main-actions/free-strike.md) [distance](../rule/combat/distance.md). You also gain the [Strike for Me](../feature/ability/summoner/level-1/strike-for-me.md) [triggered action](../rule/combat/triggered-action.md), which now has the Psionic keyword and loses the Magic keyword.

Additionally, you can summon up to two of your signature minion while outside of combat to do simple tasks.
