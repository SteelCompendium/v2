# Ringleader

---

*Don't worry. I've got a guy.*

**Prerequisite:** You complete three downtime projects during the same [respite](../rule/resource/respite.md) with the help of [followers](../rule/general/follower.md) or minions.

**Effect:** You no longer need [line of effect](../rule/combat/line-of-effect.md) to give commands to any minions you can summon. Additionally, choose one of the following benefits:

*For the Boss:* Your [followers](../rule/general/follower.md) gain a +3 bonus to [project rolls](../rule/downtime/project-roll.md) that they make.

*Networker:* Your maximum [follower](../rule/general/follower.md) count increases by 1.

*Stringpuller:* While occupying a civilized area (such as a village, town, district, or city), you always have access to a follower native to the location. If the area is otherwise hostile to you, this follower is a spy in hiding who has any project points they earn halved.
