# Stronghold

---

Many [followers](../rule/general/follower.md) stay at a [stronghold](stronghold.md), which is a home base you designate and can change. Your [stronghold](stronghold.md) is typically a location shared by your fellow heroes. It could be a few rooms at an inn in a sleepy village, an old castle you claimed after clearing it of monsters, or a fleet of sailing ships.
