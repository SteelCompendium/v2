<div class="pj__prose">
<p>You revitalize your spirit by spending time with people you love who you haven't seen in a long while. You must be able to communicate with those people to undertake this project or make <a href="../rule/downtime/project-roll/">project rolls</a> for it. When you complete this project, your <a href="../rule/health/stamina/">Stamina</a> maximum increases by 12 + your level until the end of your next <a href="../rule/resource/respite/">respite</a>, and you can't start another Spend Time With Loved Ones project for a month.</p>
<h3 id="spend-time-with-loved-ones-events-table">Spend Time With Loved Ones Events Table</h3>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>d10</th>
<th>Event</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>1</td>
<td>Before the roll, the hero is presented with an heirloom, artifact, or secret that aids another project they've undertaken or want to undertake. They can immediately make a <a href="../rule/downtime/project-roll/">project roll</a> for that project as well as for this one.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>2</td>
<td>Before the roll, the hero's people embrace their loved one's companions as their own. Additional party members can make one <a href="../rule/downtime/project-roll/">project roll</a> each for this project without doing so as a <a href="../rule/resource/respite/">respite</a> activity. When the project is completed, each character who contributed a <a href="../rule/downtime/project-roll/">project roll</a> gains the benefit of completion.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>3</td>
<td>Before the roll, the hero's enemies track them to the home or community of their loved ones. These enemies immediately launch an attack, hoping to catch the hero off guard.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>4</td>
<td>After the roll, the hero learns that a distant friend or family member has joined the cause of their enemies. Their loved ones plead with the hero to bring this person back home safely.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>5</td>
<td>After the roll, a loved one gives the hero a <a href="../rule/treasure/consumable/">consumable</a> treasure to aid them in their travels.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>6</td>
<td>After the roll, the hero's loved ones share that they have been subjected to a curse. The only way to lift the curse is by returning an heirloom to its place of origin.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>7</td>
<td>Before the roll, the hero meets an old friend who is a more experienced adventurer. They offer to mentor the hero in exchange for a treasure or favor. If the hero accepts, they also complete the Learn From a Master project when this project is complete, with the Director choosing the <a href="../rule/general/npc/">NPC</a>'s class.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>8</td>
<td>Before the roll, the hero discovers that by returning, they've put the community surrounding their loved ones in danger, either with the authorities or an opposing power in the region. To complete the project, the hero needs to earn the trust of that community.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>9</td>
<td>After the roll, the hero learns that spending time with loved ones isn't always as rejuvenating as one hopes. Completing the project requires an additional 20 <a href="../rule/downtime/project-points/">project points</a> thanks to difficult friends or relatives.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>10</td>
<td>Before the roll, the hero's loved ones ask if the hero would like to cook with them. If the hero accepts, they can apply their <a href="../rule/downtime/project-roll/">project roll</a> both to this project and to the Perfect New Recipe project. If the hero is not currently undertaking the Perfect New Recipe project, they start that project and their loved ones provide the <a href="../rule/downtime/item-prerequisite/">item prerequisite</a> and <a href="../rule/downtime/project-source/">project source</a>.</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
