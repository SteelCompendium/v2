<div class="pj__prose">
<p>When you start this project, you choose a recipe for a specific type of dish. The <a href="../rule/downtime/item-prerequisite/">item prerequisite</a> for the project is the ingredients required, which depend on the recipe and which might be difficult to acquire depending on the recipe and its place of origin. You gain a +3 <a href="../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties/">bonus</a> to the <a href="../rule/downtime/project-roll/">project roll</a> if the recipe is one from your culture.</p>
<p>When you complete the project, you can make five servings of food from the recipe as a <a href="../rule/resource/respite/">respite</a> activity whenever you have access to the ingredients. You and other creatures taking a <a href="../rule/resource/respite/">respite</a> with you can eat a serving of the food and gain its benefits (see below).</p>
<p>A creature who eats a serving of food from a particular type of recipe gains one of the following benefits, which lasts until the creature takes another <a href="../rule/resource/respite/">respite</a>.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Comforting:</strong> When the creature fails a <a href="../rule/general/saving-throw/">saving throw</a>, they can choose to succeed instead. This benefit then ends.</li>
<li><strong>Hearty:</strong> The creature increases their <a href="../rule/health/recoveries/">Recoveries</a> by 1 at the end of the <a href="../rule/resource/respite/">respite</a> in which the food is consumed.</li>
<li><strong><a href="../rule/general/supernatural/">Supernatural</a> Power:</strong> The creature temporarily increases one of their <a href="../rule/character/characteristic/">characteristic</a> scores by 1 (to a maximum of 6).</li>
</ul>
<p>A creature can benefit from the food of only one recipe at a time, and can't benefit from more than one serving of a recipe at a time.</p>
