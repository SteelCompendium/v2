# Imbue Treasure

---

You can create [leveled treasures](../rule/treasure/leveled-treasure.md) by imbuing items with magic or psionic power. You must have a mundane version of the item you plan to imbue, such as a sword or shield, when you start this project.

You can imbue any item three times. The first time you imbue it, you pick a 1st-level [enhancement](../rule/treasure/enhancement.md). The second time you imbue it, you pick a 5th-level [enhancement](../rule/treasure/enhancement.md). The third time you imbue it, you pick a 9th-level [enhancement](../rule/treasure/enhancement.md). A hero using armor or a weapon can't benefit from an [enhancement](../rule/treasure/enhancement.md) unless they are using a kit that includes the armor or weapon type, and their level matches the [enhancement](../rule/treasure/enhancement.md)'s level.

If an [enhancement](../rule/treasure/enhancement.md) has a Roman numeral greater than I after it, such as the Phasing III [enhancement](../rule/treasure/enhancement.md), that [enhancement](../rule/treasure/enhancement.md) can't be applied unless the item has all previous versions of the [enhancement](../rule/treasure/enhancement.md). This means you can't imbue armor with the Phasing III [enhancement](../rule/treasure/enhancement.md) unless it has the Phasing I and Phasing II [enhancements](../rule/treasure/enhancement.md) first. The effects of numbered [enhancements](../rule/treasure/enhancement.md) are cumulative unless otherwise noted.

[Enhancements](../rule/treasure/enhancement.md) are broken out by item type (armor, [implement](../rule/treasure/implement.md), or weapon) and level. Each item type and level features a table showing the name, [item prerequisite](../rule/downtime/item-prerequisite.md), and [project source](../rule/downtime/project-source.md) language of each [enhancement](../rule/treasure/enhancement.md). Imbued items take the Magic or Psionic keyword (your choice) and are part of any kit categories appropriate to the type of item (light armor, medium weapon, and so forth).

Imbuing treasure with [supernatural](../rule/general/supernatural.md) power as a downtime project typically means you undertake that project to make use of the treasure yourself. As such, all the features of imbuing refer to "you" as the user of the item. However, imbued items can be created for other creatures, or created by other creatures and found as treasure.

## Imbue Armor {data-scc="mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.treasure/enhancement"}

Armor imbued with an enhancement grants you special benefits while it is worn. Additionally, when your armor receives its 1st-level enhancement, it also grants a +6 [bonus](../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md) to [Stamina](../rule/health/stamina.md). A 5th-level enhancement increases the [Stamina](../rule/health/stamina.md) [bonus](../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md) to +12, and a 9th-level enhancement increases it to +21.

> **Clothing as Armor Treasures**
>
> A hero who has a kit that provides no armor, such as the [Martial Artist](../kit/martial-artist.md) or [Sniper](../kit/sniper.md) kit, can create and make use of leveled armor treasures that imbue mundane clothing with [supernatural](../rule/general/supernatural.md) power. At the Director's discretion, heroes who don't use kits, such as [elementalists](../class/elementalist.md) and [nulls](../class/null.md), can also benefit from such treasures.

### 1st-Level Armor Enhancement

**[Item Prerequisite](../rule/downtime/item-prerequisite.md):** Varies

**[Project Source](../rule/downtime/project-source.md):** Texts or lore in a language determined by the [enhancement](../rule/treasure/enhancement.md)

**[Project Roll](../rule/downtime/project-roll.md) [Characteristic](../rule/character/characteristic.md):** [Might](../rule/character/might.md), [Reason](../rule/character/reason.md), or [Intuition](../rule/character/intuition.md)

**Project Goal:** 150

**Awe:** When you start this project, choose either Charming or Threatening. If you choose Charming, you gain an [edge](../rule/dice/edge.md) on [Presence](../rule/character/presence.md) [tests](../rule/test/test.md) made to win other creatures over or make a good impression. If you choose Threatening, you gain an [edge](../rule/dice/edge.md) on [Presence](../rule/character/presence.md) [tests](../rule/test/test.md) made to intimidate, coerce, or bully.

**[Damage Immunity](../rule/damage/damage-immunity.md) I:** When you start this project, select three [damage types](../rule/damage/damage-type.md). You have immunity 5 to those [damage types](../rule/damage/damage-type.md).

**Disguise:** You can use a maneuver to cause this armor to take the form of any type of clothing that you have been in the presence of—a noble's dress, a guard's uniform, a cultist's robes, and so forth. The armor loses none of its protective qualities while transformed into other clothing.

**Iridescent:** When you are the sole target of an ability, you can use a free [triggered action](../rule/combat/triggered-action.md) to reveal that the ability was targeting an afterimage of you in the same space as you. The [power roll](../rule/dice/power-roll.md) for the ability is treated as an 11. You can't use this [enhancement](../rule/treasure/enhancement.md) again until you earn 1 or more [Victories](../rule/resource/victories.md).

**Magic Resistance I:** Your [characteristic](../rule/character/characteristic.md) scores are treated as 1 higher (to a maximum of 2) for the purpose of resisting the [potencies](../rule/character/potency.md) of magic abilities.

**Nettlebloom:** Whenever you are [grabbed](../condition/grabbed.md) by an [adjacent](../rule/combat/adjacent.md) creature, your armor sprouts toxic nettles. While that creature has you [grabbed](../condition/grabbed.md), they are [weakened](../condition/weakened.md).

**Phasing I:** Once per [turn](../rule/combat/turn.md), you can move through 1 square of solid matter. If you end your [turn](../rule/combat/turn.md) inside solid matter, you are forced out into the space from which you entered it and you take 5 damage that can't be reduced in any way.

**Psionic Resistance I:** Your [characteristic](../rule/character/characteristic.md) scores are treated as 1 higher (to a maximum of 2) for the purpose of resisting the [potencies](../rule/character/potency.md) of psionic abilities.

**Swift:** You gain a +1 [bonus](../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md) to [speed](../rule/character/speed.md).

**Tempest I:** As a maneuver, you infuse this armor with the essence of a storm. The first time an [adjacent](../rule/combat/adjacent.md) creature deals damage to you before the end of your next [turn](../rule/combat/turn.md), they take lightning damage equal to your highest [characteristic](../rule/character/characteristic.md) score and you can [push](../movement/forced-movement.md) them 1 square.

### 5th-Level Armor Enhancement

**[Item Prerequisite](../rule/downtime/item-prerequisite.md):** Armor with a 1st-level [enhancement](../rule/treasure/enhancement.md); varies

**[Project Source](../rule/downtime/project-source.md):** Texts or lore in a language determined by the [enhancement](../rule/treasure/enhancement.md)

**[Project Roll](../rule/downtime/project-roll.md) [Characteristic](../rule/character/characteristic.md):** [Might](../rule/character/might.md), [Reason](../rule/character/reason.md), or [Intuition](../rule/character/intuition.md)

**Project Goal:** 150

**Absorption:** Whenever you are targeted by a magic or psionic ability that targets only one creature, you can use a free [triggered action](../rule/combat/triggered-action.md) to cause this armor to absorb the ability after the ability's effects resolve. While the armor has an ability absorbed, you can't absorb another.

You can use an absorbed ability as if you knew it, making [power rolls](../rule/dice/power-roll.md) for the ability using your choice of [Reason](../rule/character/reason.md), [Intuition](../rule/character/intuition.md), or [Presence](../rule/character/presence.md). You don't need to spend any [Heroic Resource](../rule/resource/heroic-resource.md) to activate the ability. Once you use the ability, the armor loses it, and you can absorb another.

**[Damage Immunity](../rule/damage/damage-immunity.md) II:** The [damage immunity](../rule/damage/damage-immunity.md) conveyed by the armor increases to 10.

**Dragon Soul:** When another creature causes you to be [winded](../rule/health/winded.md) or [dying](../rule/health/dying.md), you can use a free [triggered action](../rule/combat/triggered-action.md) to cause the soul of a dragon to emerge from this armor and hurtle toward the creature. Make the following [power roll](../rule/dice/power-roll.md) against the creature.

**[Power Roll](../rule/dice/power-roll.md) + Your Highest [Characteristic](../rule/character/characteristic.md) Score:**

- **≤11:** 8 damage; [push](../movement/forced-movement.md) 3
- **12-16:** 12 damage; [push](../movement/forced-movement.md) 4
- **17+:** 15 damage; [push](../movement/forced-movement.md) 5

**Levitating:** On your [turn](../rule/combat/turn.md), you can treat up to 5 consecutive squares of movement as [flying](../movement/fly.md) movement. If you are still in midair at the end of your [turn](../rule/combat/turn.md), you [fall prone](../condition/prone.md).

**Magic Resistance II:** Your [characteristic](../rule/character/characteristic.md) scores are treated as 2 higher (to a maximum of 3) for the purpose of resisting the [potencies](../rule/character/potency.md) of magic abilities. This benefit replaces Magic Resistance I.

**Phasing II:** When you use the armor's Phasing I [enhancement](../rule/treasure/enhancement.md), you can move through 3 squares of solid matter per [turn](../rule/combat/turn.md).

**Psionic Resistance II:** Your [characteristic](../rule/character/characteristic.md) scores are treated as 2 higher (to a maximum of 3) for the purpose of resisting the [potencies](../rule/character/potency.md) of psionic abilities. This benefit replaces Psionic Resistance I.

**Reactive:** Whenever you take damage, you have [damage immunity](../rule/damage/damage-immunity.md) 2 until the end of your next [turn](../rule/combat/turn.md) after the triggering damage is resolved.

**Second Wind:** Whenever you become [winded](../rule/health/winded.md), you can use a free [triggered action](../rule/combat/triggered-action.md) to spend a [Recovery](../rule/health/recoveries.md).

**Shattering:** Whenever an enemy scores a [critical hit](../rule/combat/critical-hit.md) against you, they take 10 sonic damage.

**Tempest II:** When you use the armor's Tempest I [enhancement](../rule/treasure/enhancement.md), the affected creature takes 8 lightning damage and you [push](../movement/forced-movement.md) them up to 3 squares.

## Imbue Implement {data-scc="mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.treasure/implement"}

Implements are jewelry, spectacles, orbs, staffs, tomes, wands, weapons, and other objects used by those who channel magic and psionic power to focus that power. You decide what object to imbue when you create an implement treasure, but it must be an object you can carry or wear. You must have a mundane version of the item you plan to imbue when you start this project.

An implement imbued with an [enhancement](../rule/treasure/enhancement.md) grants you special benefits while it is wielded. Additionally, when an implement receives its 1st-level [enhancement](../rule/treasure/enhancement.md), it grants your magic or psionic abilities that deal [rolled damage](../rule/damage/rolled-damage.md) a +1 damage [bonus](../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md). A 5th-level [enhancement](../rule/treasure/enhancement.md) increases the [bonus](../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md) to +2, and a 9th-level [enhancement](../rule/treasure/enhancement.md) increases it to +3. [Censors](../class/censor.md), [conduits](../class/conduit.md), [elementalists](../class/elementalist.md), [nulls](../class/null.md), [talents](../class/talent.md), and [troubadours](../class/troubadour.md) benefit from using implements more than the other classes in this book.

### 1st-Level Implement Enhancement

**[Item Prerequisite](../rule/downtime/item-prerequisite.md):** Varies

**[Project Source](../rule/downtime/project-source.md):** Texts or lore in a language determined by the [enhancement](../rule/treasure/enhancement.md)

**[Project Roll](../rule/downtime/project-roll.md) [Characteristic](../rule/character/characteristic.md):** [Agility](../rule/character/agility.md), [Reason](../rule/character/reason.md), or [Intuition](../rule/character/intuition.md)

**Project Goal:** 150

**Berserking:** Whenever you damage a creature using a magic or psionic ability and obtain a tier 3 outcome, that creature must make an [opportunity attack](../rule/combat/opportunity-attack.md) against their nearest ally if possible after the ability's effects resolve. This [strike](../rule/combat/strike.md) deals extra damage equal to the highest of your [Reason](../rule/character/reason.md), [Intuition](../rule/character/intuition.md), or [Presence](../rule/character/presence.md) scores.

**Displacing I:** Whenever you damage a creature using a magic or psionic ability and obtain a tier 3 outcome, you can [teleport](../movement/teleport.md) that creature up to 2 squares after the ability's effects resolve. If the creature started on a horizontal surface, they must end on a horizontal surface.

**Elemental:** Whenever you use an ability with the Air, Earth, Fire, Green, Rot, Void, or Water keyword, you can attune this [implement](../rule/treasure/implement.md) to that element until the end of the encounter. While the [implement](../rule/treasure/implement.md) is attuned, you gain an [edge](../rule/dice/edge.md) on [power rolls](../rule/dice/power-roll.md) with that elemental keyword. The [implement](../rule/treasure/implement.md) can be attuned to only one element at a time.

**Forceful I:** Whenever you use a magic or psionic ability to [push](../movement/forced-movement.md) or [pull](../movement/forced-movement.md) a creature, you can move that creature an additional 2 squares.

**Rat Form:** As a maneuver, you transform into a rat. Your equipment transforms with you. As a rat, you have [speed](../rule/character/speed.md) 5 and can automatically climb at full [speed](../rule/character/speed.md) while moving, your [size](../rule/character/size.md) is 1T, and you can see in the dark. You can speak and keep your skills while in rat form, but your [Might](../rule/character/might.md) is −5 and you lose all your regular abilities, features, and benefits. You can revert to your natural form as a maneuver, and do so automatically if you take any damage.

**Rejuvenating I:** Whenever you use an ability that costs 1 or more of your [Heroic Resource](../rule/resource/heroic-resource.md), roll a d10. On a 9 or higher, you gain 1 [Heroic Resource](../rule/resource/heroic-resource.md).

**Seeking:** Your [ranged](../rule/combat/ranged.md) magic or psionic abilities gain a +2 [distance](../rule/combat/distance.md) [bonus](../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md). Additionally, if you think the name of a specific creature, place, or object to the [implement](../rule/treasure/implement.md), the [implement](../rule/treasure/implement.md) points toward that target, provided you are on the same world.

**Thought Sending:** Your [ranged](../rule/combat/ranged.md) magic and psionic abilities gain a +2 [distance](../rule/combat/distance.md) [bonus](../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md). Additionally, you can telepathically communicate with any willing creature who knows a language and whose name you know, provided they are on the same world as you. You must initiate the conversation, but once you do, the creature can respond until you end the conversation.

## Imbue Weapon

A weapon imbued with an [enhancement](../rule/treasure/enhancement.md) grants you special benefits while it is wielded. Additionally, when a weapon receives its 1st-level [enhancement](../rule/treasure/enhancement.md), it grants your weapon abilities that deal [rolled damage](../rule/damage/rolled-damage.md) a +1 damage [bonus](../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md). A 5th-level [enhancement](../rule/treasure/enhancement.md) increases the damage [bonus](../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md) to +2, and a 9th-level [enhancement](../rule/treasure/enhancement.md) increases it to +3.

### Unarmed Strike Treasures

A hero who has a kit that uses unarmed [strikes](../rule/combat/strike.md), such as the [Martial Artist](../kit/martial-artist.md) and [Pugilist](../kit/pugilist.md) kit, can create leveled weapon treasures that enhance their unarmed [strikes](../rule/combat/strike.md) by imbuing hand wraps, rings, shoes, or any other item worn on the body with [supernatural](../rule/general/supernatural.md) power. At the Director's discretion, heroes who don't use kits, such as [elementalists](../class/elementalist.md) and [nulls](../class/null.md), can also benefit from such treasures.
