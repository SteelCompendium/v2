# Heal

---

<section class="sc-trait sc-trait--lead" data-action="trait">
<header class="sc-trait__head">
<div class="sc-trait__titles">
<h3 class="sc-trait__name">Heal</h3>
</div>
</header>
<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p>A creature who uses the Heal main action employs medicine or inspiring words to make an adjacent creature feel better and stay in the fight. The target creature can spend a Recovery to regain Stamina, or can make a saving throw against one effect they are suffering that is ended by a saving throw.</p>
</div>
