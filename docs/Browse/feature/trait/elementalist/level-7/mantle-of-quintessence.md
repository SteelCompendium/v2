<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p>Your <a href="../../level-4/mantle-of-essence/">Mantle of Essence</a> feature no longer requires essence.</p>
<p>Additionally, your <a href="../../level-4/mantle-of-essence/">Mantle of Essence</a> now radiates magic that creates a calming air. Creatures in the area of the mantle&#39;s aura have their starting patience increased by 1 (to a maximum of 5) during any negotiation. While in the area, you and any ally gain an edge on tests that use the <a href="../../../../../skill/handle-animals/">Handle Animals</a> skill. If you have 5 or more Victories, the bonus to patience increases to 2 and tests that use the <a href="../../../../../skill/handle-animals/">Handle Animals</a> skill have a double edge.</p>
