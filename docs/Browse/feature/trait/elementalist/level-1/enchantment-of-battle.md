<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p>You can wear light armor and wield light weapons effectively, even though you don&#39;t have a kit. While you wear light armor, you gain a +3 bonus to Stamina, and that bonus increases by 3 at 4th, 7th, and 10th levels. While you wield a light weapon, you gain a +1 damage bonus with weapon abilities, including free strikes. You can use light armor treasures and light weapon treasures.</p>
<p>If you have a kit, you can&#39;t take this <a href="../enchantment/">enchantment</a>.</p>
