<div class="sc-ability__section">
<div class="sc-ability__section-body"><p>Psionic abilities are grouped into categories according to their effects, with each category comprising one of many talent traditions:</p></div>
</div>
<div class="sc-ability__section">
<div class="sc-ability__section-body"><p>- <b>Animapathy</b> abilities allow you to manipulate, meld, and exchange the souls of living things. - <b>Chronopathy</b> abilities allow you to view future and past events, and to manipulate time to aid allies and hinder foes. - <b>Cryokinesis</b> abilities allow you to manifest and manipulate absolute cold to enemies and the environment alike. - <b>Metamorphosis</b> abilities allow you to strengthen and manipulate your body and the bodies of others to perform preternatural exploits. - <b>Pyrokinesis</b> abilities allow you to create and manipulate fire by interacting with the potential energy found in all things. - <b>Resopathy</b> abilities allow you to manipulate matter and space to create, alter, or displace your environment and the creatures and objects in it. - <b>Telekinesis</b> abilities allow you to physically manipulate creatures and objects. - <b>Telepathy</b> abilities allow you to communicate with, read, and influence the minds of other creatures.</p></div>
</div>
<div class="sc-ability__section">
<div class="sc-ability__section-body"><p>You choose a talent tradition from the following options: chronopathy, telekinesis, or telepathy. Your talent tradition is your subclass, and your choice of tradition determines many of the features you&#39;ll gain as you gain new levels. (Other talent traditions will be featured in future products.) (*Quick Build:* Telekinesis.)</p></div>
</div>
