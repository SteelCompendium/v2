<div class="sc-ability__section-body"><p>A stoned creature is magically turning to stone. A stoned creature or a creature adjacent to them can use a maneuver to cut the encroaching stone from the stoned target&#39;s body, ending the effect and dealing damage to the target equal to twice the basilisk&#39;s Might score that can&#39;t be reduced in any way. A creature reduced to 0 Stamina while they are stoned, or by an ability that causes a creature to become stoned, is turned to stone until they are restored to life by magical means.</p></div>
