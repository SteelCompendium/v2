<p><a href="../../../../../class/shadow/">Shadow</a> colleges are secret institutions that turn ordinary folk into something else. Finding a college is the first step in a rigorous initiation process that tests the mettle of an applicant. Even those who make the cut often wash out—or are kicked out—as the master <a href="../../../../../class/shadow/">shadows</a> who teach stealth, magic, and assassination to their students are often less than gentle in their approach.</p>
<p>You graduated from a <a href="../shadow-college/">shadow college</a> chosen from the following options, each of which grants you a skill. (<em>Quick Build:</em> College of Black Ash.)</p>
<ul><li>College of Black Ash: The College of Black Ash founded the art of being a <a href="../../../../../class/shadow/">shadow</a>. Its graduates are unmatched in mobility, using sorcery to <a href="../../../../../movement/teleport/">teleport</a> around the battlefield, manipulate shadows, and summon darkness. You have the <a href="../../../../../skill/magic/">Magic</a> skill.</li><li>College of Caustic Alchemy: The College of Caustic Alchemy teaches its students recipes for the acids, bombs, and poisons used in their grim work. Graduates of the college are exceptional assassins. You have the <a href="../../../../../skill/alchemy/">Alchemy</a> skill.</li><li>College of the Harlequin Mask: Graduates of the College of the Harlequin Mask learn illusion magic, which they use to infiltrate enemy strongholds and create orchestrated chaos in combat. You have the <a href="../../../../../skill/lie/">Lie</a> skill.</li></ul>
<p>Your <a href="../shadow-college/">shadow college</a> is your subclass, and your choice of college determines many of the features you&#39;ll gain as you gain new levels.</p>
