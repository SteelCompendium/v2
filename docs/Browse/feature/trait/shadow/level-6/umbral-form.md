<p class="sc-trait__leadin"><span class="sc-trait__dia"></span>As a maneuver, you lose control of yourself, becoming a shadow creature dripping with ash. <ul><li>You can automatically climb at full speed while moving.</li><li>Enemies&#39; spaces don&#39;t count as <a href="../../../../../movement/difficult-terrain/">difficult terrain</a> for you.
