# Memonek Traits

---

<section class="sc-trait sc-trait--crest" data-action="trait" data-sub="3">
<header class="sc-trait__head">
<span class="sc-crest sc-trait__crest"><span class="sc-trait__glyph">*</span></span>
<div class="sc-trait__titles">
<div class="sc-trait__eyebrow"><span class="sc-trait__dia"></span>Memonek Trait</div>
<h3 class="sc-trait__name">Memonek Traits</h3>
</div>
</header>
<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p class="sc-trait__leadin"><span class="sc-trait__dia"></span><a href="../../../../ancestry/memonek/">Memonek</a> heroes have access to the following traits.</p>
<div class="sc-trait__nest">
<section class="sc-trait" data-action="trait">
<header class="sc-trait__head">
<div class="sc-trait__titles">
<h3 class="sc-trait__name">Signature Trait: Fall Lightly</h3>
</div>
</header>
<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p>Your silicone body is low in density. Whenever you fall, you reduce the distance of the fall by 2 squares.</p>
</div>
</section>
<section class="sc-trait" data-action="trait">
<header class="sc-trait__head">
<div class="sc-trait__titles">
<h3 class="sc-trait__name">Signature Trait: Lightweight</h3>
</div>
</header>
<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p>Your body is light for a creature of your height. Whenever another creature attempts to force move you, you treat your size as one size smaller than it is.</p>
</div>
</section>
<section class="sc-trait" data-action="trait">
<header class="sc-trait__head">
<div class="sc-trait__titles">
<h3 class="sc-trait__name">Purchased Memonek Traits</h3>
</div>
</header>
<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p class="sc-trait__leadin"><span class="sc-trait__dia"></span>You have 4 ancestry points to spend on the following traits. (<em>Quick Build:</em> Lightning Nimbleness, Nonstop.)</p>
<div class="sc-trait__nest">
<section class="sc-trait" data-action="trait">
<header class="sc-trait__head">
<div class="sc-trait__titles">
<h3 class="sc-trait__name">I Am Law</h3>
</div>
</header>
<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p>Your lawful nature and quick reflexes mean you give no quarter to creatures trying to get past you. Enemies can&#39;t move through your space unless you allow them to do so.</p>
</div>
</section>
<section class="sc-trait" data-action="trait">
<header class="sc-trait__head">
<div class="sc-trait__titles">
<h3 class="sc-trait__name">Keeper of Order</h3>
</div>
</header>
<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p>Your connection to Axiom, the plane of Uttermost Law, allows you to manage chaos around you. Once per round when you or an adjacent creature makes a power roll, you can use a free triggered action to remove an edge or a bane on the roll, to turn a double edge into an edge, or to turn a double bane into a bane.</p>
</div>
</section>
<section class="sc-trait" data-action="trait">
<header class="sc-trait__head">
<div class="sc-trait__titles">
<h3 class="sc-trait__name">Lightning Nimbleness</h3>
</div>
</header>
<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p>You can push your body to move at incredible speeds. Your speed is 7.</p>
</div>
</section>
<section class="sc-trait" data-action="trait">
<header class="sc-trait__head">
<div class="sc-trait__titles">
<h3 class="sc-trait__name">Nonstop</h3>
</div>
</header>
<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p>Your connection to Axiom allows you to regulate your movement. You can&#39;t be made <a href="../../../../condition/slowed/">slowed</a>.</p>
</div>
</section>
<section class="sc-trait" data-action="trait">
<header class="sc-trait__head">
<div class="sc-trait__titles">
<h3 class="sc-trait__name">Systematic Mind</h3>
</div>
</header>
<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p>You gain an edge on tests made to parse schematics, maps, and other systematic documents that aren&#39;t inherently chaotic. In addition, you treat any language you don&#39;t know as if you know a related language.</p>
</div>
</section>
<section class="sc-trait" data-action="trait">
<header class="sc-trait__head">
<div class="sc-trait__titles">
<h3 class="sc-trait__name">Unphased</h3>
</div>
</header>
<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p>Your ordered mind can&#39;t be caught off guard. You can&#39;t be made surprised.</p>
</div>
</section>
<section class="sc-trait" data-action="trait">
<header class="sc-trait__head">
<div class="sc-trait__titles">
<h3 class="sc-trait__name">Useful Emotion</h3>
</div>
</header>
<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p>Velloparatha—the worldsickness—might hinder you, but you know how to turn your pain into something your enemies feel. At the start of any combat, you gain 1 <a href="../../../../rule/resource/surge/">surge</a>.</p>
</div>
</section>
