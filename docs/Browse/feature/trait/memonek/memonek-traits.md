# Memonek Traits

<h3 class="sc-ability__name">Memonek Traits</h3>
</div>
<div class="sc-ability__corner"></div>
</div>
<div class="sc-ability__section">
<div class="sc-ability__section-body"><p>Memonek heroes have access to the following traits.</p></div>
</div>
<div class="sc-ability__section">
<div class="sc-ability__section-body"><p>## Signature Trait: Fall Lightly {data-scc=&#34;mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.trait.memonek/fall-lightly&#34;}</p></div>
</div>
<div class="sc-ability__section">
<div class="sc-ability__section-body"><p>Your silicone body is low in density. Whenever you fall, you reduce the distance of the fall by 2 squares.</p></div>
</div>
<div class="sc-ability__section">
<div class="sc-ability__section-body"><p>## Signature Trait: Lightweight {data-scc=&#34;mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.trait.memonek/lightweight&#34;}</p></div>
</div>
<div class="sc-ability__section">
<div class="sc-ability__section-body"><p>Your body is light for a creature of your height. Whenever another creature attempts to force move you, you treat your size as one size smaller than it is.</p></div>
</div>
<div class="sc-ability__section">
<div class="sc-ability__section-body"><p>## Purchased Memonek Traits</p></div>
</div>
<div class="sc-ability__section">
<div class="sc-ability__section-body"><p>You have 4 ancestry points to spend on the following traits. (*Quick Build:* Lightning Nimbleness, Nonstop.)</p></div>
</div>
<div class="sc-ability__section">
<div class="sc-ability__section-body"><p>### I Am Law {data-scc=&#34;mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.trait.memonek/i-am-law&#34;}</p></div>
</div>
<div class="sc-ability__section">
<div class="sc-ability__section-body"><p>Your lawful nature and quick reflexes mean you give no quarter to creatures trying to get past you. Enemies can&#39;t move through your space unless you allow them to do so.</p></div>
</div>
<div class="sc-ability__section">
<div class="sc-ability__section-body"><p>### Keeper of Order {data-scc=&#34;mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.trait.memonek/keeper-of-order&#34;}</p></div>
</div>
<div class="sc-ability__section">
<div class="sc-ability__section-body"><p>Your connection to Axiom, the plane of Uttermost Law, allows you to manage chaos around you. Once per round when you or an adjacent creature makes a power roll, you can use a free triggered action to remove an edge or a bane on the roll, to turn a double edge into an edge, or to turn a double bane into a bane.</p></div>
</div>
<div class="sc-ability__section">
<div class="sc-ability__section-body"><p>### Lightning Nimbleness {data-scc=&#34;mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.trait.memonek/lightning-nimbleness&#34;}</p></div>
</div>
<div class="sc-ability__section">
<div class="sc-ability__section-body"><p>You can push your body to move at incredible speeds. Your speed is 7.</p></div>
</div>
<div class="sc-ability__section">
<div class="sc-ability__section-body"><p>### Nonstop {data-scc=&#34;mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.trait.memonek/nonstop&#34;}</p></div>
</div>
<div class="sc-ability__section">
<div class="sc-ability__section-body"><p>Your connection to Axiom allows you to regulate your movement. You can&#39;t be made slowed.</p></div>
</div>
<div class="sc-ability__section">
<div class="sc-ability__section-body"><p>### Systematic Mind {data-scc=&#34;mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.trait.memonek/systematic-mind&#34;}</p></div>
</div>
<div class="sc-ability__section">
<div class="sc-ability__section-body"><p>You gain an edge on tests made to parse schematics, maps, and other systematic documents that aren&#39;t inherently chaotic. In addition, you treat any language you don&#39;t know as if you know a related language.</p></div>
</div>
<div class="sc-ability__section">
<div class="sc-ability__section-body"><p>### Unphased {data-scc=&#34;mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.trait.memonek/unphased&#34;}</p></div>
</div>
<div class="sc-ability__section">
<div class="sc-ability__section-body"><p>Your ordered mind can&#39;t be caught off guard. You can&#39;t be made surprised.</p></div>
</div>
<div class="sc-ability__section">
<div class="sc-ability__section-body"><p>### Useful Emotion {data-scc=&#34;mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.trait.memonek/useful-emotion&#34;}</p></div>
</div>
<div class="sc-ability__section">
<div class="sc-ability__section-body"><p>Velloparatha—the worldsickness—might hinder you, but you know how to turn your pain into something your enemies feel. At the start of any combat, you gain 1 surge.</p></div>
</div>
