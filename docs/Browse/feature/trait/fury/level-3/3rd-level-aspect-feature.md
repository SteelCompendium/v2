Additionally, you automatically sense the presence of animals and elementals within 10 squares of you, even if they are hidden.</p><p>When you are in a negotiation with an animal or elemental, you treat your Renown as 1 higher than usual. This stacks with the increase to your effective Renown in a negotiation with an animal of your type while in <a href="../../../../../complication/animal-form/">animal form</a> (see <a href="../../stormwight-kits/">Stormwight Kits</a>).</p><p>## See Through Their Tricks {data-scc=&#34;mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.trait.fury.level-3/see-through-their-tricks&#34;}</p><p>You have a double edge on tests made to search for hidden creatures, discern hidden motives, or detect lies. You also have a double edge on tests made to gamble!</p></div>
