<section class="sc-trait sc-trait--lead" data-action="trait">

<h3 class="sc-trait__name">Primordial Aspect</h3>

<div class="sc-trait__tag">Level <span class="num">1</span></div>
</header>
<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p>You are a product of customs older than warfare, older than civilization, older than most of the world. You have undergone a rite of passage that revealed the building blocks of the timescape—the Primordial Chaos—and that left an aspect of that chaos inside you. You choose a <a href="../primordial-aspect/">primordial aspect</a> from the following options, each of which grants you a skill. (<em>Quick Build:</em> Berserker.)</p>
<ul><li><b>Berserker:</b> You channel your ferocity into physical might, acting as a living version of the forces that shape the world. You have the <a href="../../../../../skill/lift/">Lift</a> skill.</li><li><b>Reaver:</b> You channel your ferocity into instinct and cunning, challenging the order of civilization. You have the <a href="../../../../../skill/hide/">Hide</a> skill.</li><li><b>Stormwight:</b> You channel your ferocity into primordial storms and can take on the form of an animal or an animal hybrid form. You have the <a href="../../../../../skill/track/">Track</a> skill.</li></ul>
<p>Your <a href="../primordial-aspect/">primordial aspect</a> is your subclass, and your choice of aspect determines many of the features you&#39;ll gain as you gain new levels.</p>
</div>
