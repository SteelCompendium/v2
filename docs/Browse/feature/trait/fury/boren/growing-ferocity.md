<h3 class="sc-trait__name">Growing Ferocity</h3>

<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p>As your ferocity grows, you gain benefits as noted on the <a href="../../../../../kit/boren/">Boren</a> <a href="../growing-ferocity/">Growing Ferocity</a> table. Benefits are cumulative except where an improved benefit replaces a lesser benefit.</p>
