<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p>Whenever you use your <a href="../../../../ability/null/level-1/inertial-shield/">Inertial Shield</a> ability, you can then use the Grab maneuver as a free triggered action.</p>
<p>Additionally, as your discipline grows, you strengthen the psionic power suffusing you, granting benefits from the <a href="../cryokinetic-mastery/">Cryokinetic Mastery</a> table. Benefits are cumulative except where an improved benefit replaces a lesser benefit.</p>
