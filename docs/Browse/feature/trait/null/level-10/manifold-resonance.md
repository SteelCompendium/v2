<p>Your body becomes perfected matter, beyond the whims and chaos of the timescape and the restrictions of the manifolds. Each time you finish a respite, you can <a href="../../../../../movement/shifting/">shift</a> yourself and any creatures in the area of your <a href="../../level-1/null-field/">Null Field</a> ability to any location in the timescape known to you, known to any other creature in the area, or where any supernatural treasure in the area has been before.</p>
<p>Whenever you use an ability, you gain 1 discipline that can be used only to apply a benefit from your <a href="../../level-7/psi-boost/">Psi Boost</a> feature to that ability. Additionally, you and allies in the area of your <a href="../../level-1/null-field/">Null Field</a> ability ignore banes and double banes on your power rolls.</p>
