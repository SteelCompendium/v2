<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p>You have an epic resource called <a href="../applause/">applause</a>. Each time you finish a respite, you gain <a href="../applause/">applause</a> equal to the XP you gain. You can spend <a href="../applause/">applause</a> on your abilities as if it were drama.</p>
<p>Additionally, whenever you or a creature within 3 squares would obtain a failure or a tier 1 outcome on a test, you can spend 1 <a href="../applause/">applause</a> to improve the outcome by 1 tier.</p>
<p><a href="../applause/">Applause</a> remains until you spend it.</p>
