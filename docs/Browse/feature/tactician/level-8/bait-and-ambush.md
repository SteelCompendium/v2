<p>You have trained your squad to be silent ambushers.</p>
<p><b>Mark Benefit:</b> When you or any ally makes a <a href="../../../../rule/combat/strike/">strike</a> against a creature marked by you, you can spend 2 focus to let the character making the <a href="../../../../rule/combat/strike/">strike</a> <a href="../../../../movement/shifting/">shift</a> up to a number of squares equal to your <a href="../../../../rule/character/reason/">Reason</a> score and use the <a href="../../../common/maneuvers/hide/">Hide</a> maneuver as a <a href="../../../../rule/combat/free-maneuver/">free maneuver</a> once during the <a href="../../../../movement/shifting/">shift</a>. The creature can <a href="../../../../movement/shifting/">shift</a> before or after the <a href="../../../../rule/combat/strike/">strike</a> is resolved.</p>
