# Judgment

---

<section class="sc-trait sc-trait--crest" data-action="trait" data-sub="2">
<header class="sc-head"><div class="sc-head__stack"><span class="sc-crest sc-trait__crest"><span class="sc-trait__glyph">*</span></span>
<div class="sc-head__col sc-head__col--left"><div class="sc-head__slot sc-head__left-eyebrow sc-head__slot--line">Feature</div><h3 class="sc-head__slot sc-head__left-primary sc-head__slot--line">Judgment</h3><div class="sc-head__slot sc-head__left-deck sc-head__slot--line">Censor</div></div></div><div class="sc-head__rail sc-head__col--right"><div class="sc-head__slot sc-head__right-eyebrow sc-head__slot--chip">Level 1</div></div></header>
<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p class="sc-trait__leadin"><span class="sc-trait__dia"></span>You pick out the enemies most worthy of your wrath and place a divine <a href="../../../ability/censor/level-1/judgment/">judgment</a> upon them, censoring them with the power of your god. You have the following ability.</p>
<div class="sc-trait__nest">
<article class="sc-ability sc-fil" data-action="maneuver">
<header class="sc-head"><div class="sc-head__stack"><span class="sc-crest sc-ability__crest"><span class="sc-ability__glyph">f</span></span><div class="sc-head__col sc-head__col--left"><div class="sc-head__slot sc-head__left-eyebrow sc-head__slot--line">Ability</div><h3 class="sc-head__slot sc-head__left-primary sc-head__slot--line">Judgment</h3></div></div><div class="sc-head__rail sc-head__col--right"><div class="sc-head__slot sc-head__right-eyebrow sc-head__slot--chip">Level 1</div><div class="sc-head__slot sc-head__right-deck sc-head__slot--chip">Maneuver</div></div></header>
<p class="sc-ability__flavor">You utter a prayer that outlines your foe in holy energy.</p>
<div class="sc-ability__kw"><span class="sc-ability__chip">Magic</span><span class="sc-ability__chip"><a href="../../../../rule/combat/ranged/">Ranged</a></span></div>
<div class="sc-ability__rail">
<div class="sc-ability__cell"><div class="l">Distance</div><div class="v"><a href="../../../../rule/combat/ranged/">Ranged</a> 10</div></div>
<div class="sc-ability__cell"><div class="l">Targets</div><div class="v">One enemy</div></div>
</div>
<div class="sc-ability__section">
<div class="sc-ability__section-head"><span class="sc-ability__dia"></span><span class="tag">Effect</span></div>
<div class="sc-ability__section-body"><p>The target is judged by you until the end of the encounter, you use this ability again, you willingly end this effect (no action required), or another <a href="../../../../class/censor/">censor</a> judges the target.</p><p>Whenever a creature judged by you uses a main action and is within your <a href="../../../../rule/combat/line-of-effect/">line of effect</a>, you can use a free <a href="../../../../rule/combat/triggered-action/">triggered action</a> to deal holy damage equal to twice your <a href="../../../../rule/character/presence/">Presence</a> score to them.</p><p>When a creature judged by you is reduced to 0 <a href="../../../../rule/health/stamina/">Stamina</a>, you can use a free <a href="../../../../rule/combat/triggered-action/">triggered action</a> to use this ability against a new target.</p><p>Additionally, you can spend 1 wrath to take one of the following free <a href="../../../../rule/combat/triggered-action/">triggered actions</a>:</p><ul><li>When an <a href="../../../../rule/combat/adjacent/">adjacent</a> creature judged by you starts to <a href="../../../../movement/shifting/">shift</a>, you make a <a href="../../../../rule/combat/melee/">melee</a> <a href="../../../common/main-actions/free-strike/">free strike</a> against them and their <a href="../../../../rule/character/speed/">speed</a> becomes 0 until the end of the current <a href="../../../../rule/combat/turn/">turn</a>, preventing them from <a href="../../../../movement/shifting/">shifting</a>.</li><li>When a creature judged by you within 10 squares makes a <a href="../../../../rule/dice/power-roll/">power roll</a>, you cause them to take a <a href="../../../../rule/dice/bane/">bane</a> on the roll.</li><li>When a creature judged by you within 10 squares uses an ability with a <a href="../../../../rule/character/potency/">potency</a> that targets only one creature, the <a href="../../../../rule/character/potency/">potency</a> is reduced by 1 for that creature.</li><li>If you damage a creature judged by you with a <a href="../../../../rule/combat/melee/">melee</a> ability, the creature is <a href="../../../../condition/taunted/">taunted</a> by you until the end of their next <a href="../../../../rule/combat/turn/">turn</a>.</li></ul><p>You can choose only one free <a href="../../../../rule/combat/triggered-action/">triggered action</a> option at a time, even if multiple options are triggered by the same effect.</p></div>
</div>
</article>
<section class="sc-trait" data-action="trait">
<header class="sc-head"><div class="sc-head__stack"><div class="sc-head__col sc-head__col--left"><h3 class="sc-head__slot sc-head__left-primary sc-head__slot--line">Judgment Order Benefit</h3></div></div><div class="sc-head__rail sc-head__col--right"><div class="sc-head__slot sc-head__right-eyebrow sc-head__slot--chip">Level 1</div></div></header>
<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p class="sc-trait__leadin"><span class="sc-trait__dia"></span>The first time on a <a href="../../../../rule/combat/turn/">turn</a> that you use your <a href="../../../ability/censor/level-1/judgment/">Judgment</a> ability to judge a creature, you gain the following benefit based on your order:</p>
<ul><li><b>Exorcist:</b> You can <a href="../../../../movement/teleport/">teleport</a> up to a number of squares equal to twice your <a href="../../../../rule/character/presence/">Presence</a> score. This movement must take you closer to the judged creature. You do not need <a href="../../../../rule/combat/line-of-effect/">line of effect</a> to your destination.</li><li><b>Oracle:</b> You can deal holy damage equal to twice your <a href="../../../../rule/character/presence/">Presence</a> score to the judged creature.</li><li><b>Paragon:</b> You can vertical <a href="../../../../movement/forced-movement/">pull</a> the judged creature up to a number of squares equal to twice your <a href="../../../../rule/character/presence/">Presence</a> score.</li></ul>
</div>
