<div class="sc-ability__section-body"><p>The target is judged by you until the end of the encounter, you use this ability again, you willingly end this effect (no action required), or another <a href="../../../../class/censor/">censor</a> judges the target.</p><p>Whenever a creature judged by you uses a main action and is within your line of effect, you can use a free triggered action to deal holy damage equal to twice your Presence score to them.</p><p>When a creature judged by you is reduced to 0 Stamina, you can use a free triggered action to use this ability against a new target.</p><p>Additionally, you can spend 1 wrath to take one of the following free triggered actions:</p><ul><li>When an adjacent creature judged by you starts to <a href="../../../../movement/shifting/">shift</a>, you make a melee <a href="../../../common/main-actions/free-strike/">free strike</a> against them and their speed becomes 0 until the end of the current turn, preventing them from <a href="../../../../movement/shifting/">shifting</a>.</li><li>When a creature judged by you within 10 squares makes a power roll, you cause them to take a bane on the roll.</li><li>When a creature judged by you within 10 squares uses an ability with a potency that targets only one creature, the potency is reduced by 1 for that creature.</li><li>If you damage a creature judged by you with a melee ability, the creature is <a href="../../../../condition/taunted/">taunted</a> by you until the end of their next turn.</li></ul><p>You can choose only one free triggered action option at a time, even if multiple options are triggered by the same effect.</p></div>
