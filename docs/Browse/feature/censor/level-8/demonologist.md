<p>The most esoteric secrets of your order teach you that to defeat your enemy, you must understand them. You treat your <a href="../../../../rule/resource/renown/">Renown</a> as 2 higher than usual when dealing with demons, <a href="../../../../ancestry/devil/">devils</a>, and other agents of chaos. If you successfully complete a negotiation with one of these creatures, you gain an <a href="../../../../rule/dice/edge/">edge</a> on <a href="../../../../rule/dice/power-roll/">power rolls</a> made against them and can use your <a href="../../../ability/censor/level-1/judgment/">Judgment</a> ability against them as a free <a href="../../../../rule/combat/triggered-action/">triggered action</a> before an encounter begins.</p>
