# Templar

---

<section class="sc-trait sc-trait--crest sc-trait--lead" data-action="trait">
<header class="sc-head"><div class="sc-head__stack"><span class="sc-crest sc-trait__crest"><span class="sc-trait__glyph">*</span></span>
<div class="sc-head__col sc-head__col--left"><div class="sc-head__slot sc-head__left-eyebrow sc-head__slot--line">Feature</div><h3 class="sc-head__slot sc-head__left-primary sc-head__slot--line">Templar</h3><div class="sc-head__slot sc-head__left-deck sc-head__slot--line">Censor</div></div></div><div class="sc-head__rail sc-head__col--right"><div class="sc-head__slot sc-head__right-eyebrow sc-head__slot--chip">Level 10</div></div></header>
<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p>You are the ultimate representation of your god&#39;s justice in the timescape. Whenever you use your <a href="../../../ability/censor/level-1/judgment/">Judgment</a> ability, you can use a free <a href="../../../../rule/combat/triggered-action/">triggered action</a> to use a <a href="../../../../class/conduit/">conduit</a> domain effect (see <a href="../../../conduit/level-1/domain-piety-and-effects/">Domain Piety and Effects</a> in the <a href="../../../../class/conduit/">Conduit</a> section) associated with your chosen domain, or a domain you access with <a href="../virtue/">virtue</a> (see below). If the effect calls for the use of your <a href="../../../../rule/character/intuition/">Intuition</a> score, you use your <a href="../../../../rule/character/presence/">Presence</a> score instead. If the effect uses your <a href="../../../../class/conduit/">conduit</a> level, use your <a href="../../../../class/censor/">censor</a> level instead.</p>
<p>Additionally, whenever you take a <a href="../../../../rule/resource/respite/">respite</a>, you can open a portal to rest in the presence of your deity and bring along any allies. When you do, you can ask your deity three questions, which the Director must answer honestly if your deity knows the answers (though they might answer cryptically or incompletely). When you finish your <a href="../../../../rule/resource/respite/">respite</a>, you and your allies can appear at any location in the timescape where someone worships your deity.</p>
<p>While you rest in their presence, your god might also give you priority targets to enact justice upon. You and your allies each have a double <a href="../../../../rule/dice/edge/">edge</a> on <a href="../../../../rule/dice/power-roll/">power rolls</a> made against such targets. If you attempt to open a portal to your deity again before you have defeated your priority targets, you suffer your god&#39;s wrath, as determined by the Director.</p>
</div>
</section>
