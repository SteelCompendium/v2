# Look On My Work and Despair

---

<section class="sc-trait sc-trait--crest sc-trait--lead" data-action="trait">

<h3 class="sc-trait__name">Look On My Work and Despair</h3>
</div>
<div class="sc-trait__tag">Level <span class="num">3</span></div>
</header>
<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p>Your <a href="../../../ability/censor/level-1/judgment/">judgment</a> has grown in <a href="../../../conduit/level-10/divine-power/">divine power</a>, instilling fear in those you condemn. Whenever you use your <a href="../../../ability/censor/level-1/judgment/">Judgment</a> ability, you can spend 1 wrath, and if the target has P &lt; AVERAGE, they are <a href="../../../../condition/frightened/">frightened</a> of you (save ends). Additionally, whenever a creature judged by you is reduced to 0 <a href="../../../../rule/health/stamina/">Stamina</a> and you use <a href="../../../ability/censor/level-1/judgment/">Judgment</a> as a free <a href="../../../../rule/combat/triggered-action/">triggered action</a>, if the new target has P &lt; STRONG, they are <a href="../../../../condition/frightened/">frightened</a> of you (save ends). If the target is already <a href="../../../../condition/frightened/">frightened</a> of you, they instead take holy damage equal to twice your <a href="../../../../rule/character/presence/">Presence</a> score.</p>
</div>
