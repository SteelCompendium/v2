<div class="sc-ability__section-body"><p>You jump up to your <a href="../../../../rule/character/speed/">speed</a> without provoking <a href="../../../../rule/combat/opportunity-attack/">opportunity attacks</a>. Until the end of your next <a href="../../../../rule/combat/turn/">turn</a>, a static afterimage of you remains in the space you left, and any enemy <a href="../../../../rule/combat/adjacent/">adjacent</a> to your afterimage takes a <a href="../../../../rule/dice/bane/">bane</a> on <a href="../../../../rule/dice/ability-roll/">ability rolls</a>. You can use your abilities from your own space or from the space of your afterimage as if you were still there. Additionally, if your <a href="../../level-1/null-field/">Null Field</a> ability is active, your afterimage also projects the <a href="../../../../rule/combat/aura/">aura</a> from that ability, which you control as if you were in the afterimage&#39;s space.</p></div>
