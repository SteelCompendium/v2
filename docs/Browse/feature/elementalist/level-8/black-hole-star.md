<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p>You warp gravity around your heavenly body and can pull even the sturdiest titans toward your core. At the end of each of your turns, you target one creature or object within <a href="../../../../rule/combat/distance/">distance</a> of your <a href="../../../ability/elementalist/level-1/hurl-element/">Hurl Element</a> ability and vertical pull that target up to 5 squares. If their stability reduces this <a href="../../../../movement/forced-movement/">forced movement</a>, they are pulled a minimum of 2 squares. This <a href="../../../../movement/forced-movement/">forced movement</a> ignores stability for your allies.</p>
<p>Additionally, your <a href="../../level-4/mantle-of-essence/">Mantle of Essence</a> improves. While in the area of the aura, enemies and objects have their stability reduced by an amount equal to your level.</p>
</div>
