# Summoner's Dominion

---

Your circle allows you to call forth a piece of your [portfolio's](../level-1/portfolio.md) dominion like any [minion](../level-1/minions.md).

Once per encounter, you can use a maneuver to summon a fixture from your [minions'](../level-1/minions.md) native manifold or origin into an unoccupied space on the ground within your Summoner's Range. You can spend 1 [essence](../level-1/essence.md) to relocate the fixture as a [free maneuver](../../../rule/combat/free-maneuver.md) on your [turn](../../../rule/combat/turn.md). The fixture stays until the end of the encounter, until its [Stamina](../../../rule/health/stamina.md) is reduced to 0, or until you become [dying](../../../rule/health/dying.md).

Your fixture gains additional features at 5th and 9th level.
