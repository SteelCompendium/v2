<section class="sc-trait" data-action="trait">
<header class="sc-trait__head">
<div class="sc-trait__titles">
<h3 class="sc-trait__name">Summoner&#39;s Dominion</h3>
</div>
<div class="sc-trait__tag">Level <span class="num">2</span></div>
</header>
<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p>Your circle allows you to call forth a piece of your portfolio&#39;s dominion like any minion.</p>
<p>Once per encounter, you can use a maneuver to summon a fixture from your minions&#39; native manifold or origin into an unoccupied space on the ground within your Summoner&#39;s Range. You can spend 1 essence to relocate the fixture as a free maneuver on your turn. The fixture stays until the end of the encounter, until its Stamina is reduced to 0, or until you become dying.</p>
<p>Your fixture gains additional features at 5th and 9th level.</p>
