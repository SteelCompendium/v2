<section class="sc-trait" data-action="trait">
<header class="sc-trait__head">
<div class="sc-trait__titles">
<h3 class="sc-trait__name">Summoner&#39;s Dominion</h3>
</div>
<div class="sc-trait__tag">Level <span class="num">2</span></div>
</header>
<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p>Your circle allows you to call forth a piece of your <a href="../../level-1/portfolio/">portfolio&#39;s</a> dominion like any <a href="../../level-1/minions/">minion</a>.</p>
<p>Once per encounter, you can use a maneuver to summon a fixture from your <a href="../../level-1/minions/">minions&#39;</a> native manifold or origin into an unoccupied space on the ground within your Summoner&#39;s Range. You can spend 1 <a href="../../level-1/essence/">essence</a> to relocate the fixture as a <a href="../../../../rule/combat/free-maneuver/">free maneuver</a> on your <a href="../../../../rule/combat/turn/">turn</a>. The fixture stays until the end of the encounter, until its <a href="../../../../rule/health/stamina/">Stamina</a> is reduced to 0, or until you become <a href="../../../../rule/health/dying/">dying</a>.</p>
<p>Your fixture gains additional features at 5th and 9th level.</p>
