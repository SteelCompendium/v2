# 3rd-Level Features

---

<section class="sc-trait sc-trait--crest sc-trait--lead" data-action="trait" data-sub="2">
<header class="sc-trait__head">
<span class="sc-crest sc-trait__crest"><span class="sc-trait__glyph">*</span></span>
<div class="sc-trait__titles">
<div class="sc-trait__eyebrow"><span class="sc-trait__dia"></span>Summoner Trait</div>
<h3 class="sc-trait__name">3rd-Level Features</h3>
</div>
<div class="sc-trait__tag">Level <span class="num">3</span></div>
</header>
<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p>As a 3rd-level <a href="../../../../class/summoner/">summoner</a>, you gain the following features.</p>
<div class="sc-trait__nest">
<section class="sc-trait" data-action="trait">
<header class="sc-trait__head">
<div class="sc-trait__titles">
<h3 class="sc-trait__name">Summoner&#39;s Kit</h3>
</div>
<div class="sc-trait__tag">Level <span class="num">3</span></div>
</header>
<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p class="sc-trait__leadin"><span class="sc-trait__dia"></span>You conjure a kit for yourself. This kit includes an <a href="../../../../rule/treasure/implement/">implement</a>, such as a rod or a baton, which grants you the following benefits:</p>
<ul><li>The <a href="../../../../rule/damage/damage/">damage</a> of your <a href="../../../ability/summoner/level-1/summoner-strike/">Summoner Strike</a> ability increases to twice your <a href="../../../../rule/character/reason/">Reason</a> score.</li><li>The <a href="../../../../rule/character/potency/">potency</a> of your <a href="../../../ability/summoner/level-1/summoner-strike/">Summoner Strike</a> ability increases to R &lt; AVERAGE.</li><li>The distance of your <a href="../../../ability/summoner/level-1/summoner-strike/">Summoner Strike</a> ability is now equal to your Summoner&#39;s Range.</li></ul>
<p>Your kit also comes with wards like magic armor and transient <a href="../../level-1/minions/">minion</a> forces. Choose one of the following wards.</p>
<div class="sc-trait__nest">
<section class="sc-trait" data-action="trait">
<header class="sc-trait__head">
<div class="sc-trait__titles">
<h3 class="sc-trait__name">Conjured Ward</h3>
</div>
<div class="sc-trait__tag">Level <span class="num">3</span></div>
</header>
<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p>You are clad in the natural defenses of your <a href="../../level-1/portfolio/">portfolio</a> (bones, fairy wood, stone, writhing flesh). You gain a +3 bonus to <a href="../../../../rule/health/stamina/">Stamina</a> and that bonus increases by 3 at 4th, 7th, and 10th levels.</p>
</div>
</section>
<section class="sc-trait" data-action="trait">
<header class="sc-trait__head">
<div class="sc-trait__titles">
<h3 class="sc-trait__name">Emergency Ward</h3>
</div>
<div class="sc-trait__tag">Level <span class="num">3</span></div>
</header>
<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p>The first time each <a href="../../../../rule/combat/combat-round/">round</a> you take <a href="../../../../rule/damage/damage/">damage</a>, you can use a <a href="../../../../rule/combat/triggered-action/">free triggered action</a> to <a href="../../../../movement/shifting/">shift</a> 1 after the triggering effect resolves and summon a signature <a href="../../level-1/minions/">minion</a> into the square you left (as long as there is enough space).</p>
</div>
</section>
<section class="sc-trait" data-action="trait">
<header class="sc-trait__head">
<div class="sc-trait__titles">
<h3 class="sc-trait__name">Howling Ward</h3>
</div>
<div class="sc-trait__tag">Level <span class="num">3</span></div>
</header>
<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p>You create a 1-<a href="../../../../rule/combat/aura/">aura</a> vortex of slicing magic around you when you enter combat. An enemy that starts their <a href="../../../../rule/combat/turn/">turn</a> <a href="../../../../rule/combat/adjacent/">adjacent</a> to you takes <a href="../../../../rule/damage/damage/">damage</a> equal to your <a href="../../../../rule/character/reason/">Reason</a>.</p>
</div>
</section>
<section class="sc-trait" data-action="trait">
<header class="sc-trait__head">
<div class="sc-trait__titles">
<h3 class="sc-trait__name">Snare Ward</h3>
</div>
<div class="sc-trait__tag">Level <span class="num">3</span></div>
</header>
<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p>Whenever an <a href="../../../../rule/combat/adjacent/">adjacent</a> creature deals <a href="../../../../rule/damage/damage/">damage</a> to you, you can use a <a href="../../../../rule/combat/triggered-action/">free triggered action</a> to pull that creature toward one of your <a href="../../level-1/minions/">minions</a> within your Summoner&#39;s Range a number of squares equal to your <a href="../../../../rule/character/reason/">Reason</a> score.</p>
</div>
</section>
</div>
</div>
</section>
