# Prayer of Soldier's Skill

---

<section class="sc-trait sc-trait--crest sc-trait--lead" data-action="trait">

<h3 class="sc-trait__name">Prayer of Soldier&#39;s Skill</h3>
</div>
<div class="sc-trait__tag">Level <span class="num">1</span></div>
</header>
<div class="sc-trait__body">
<p>Your god gives your mind the training of a soldier. You can wear light armor and wield light weapons effectively, even though you don&#39;t have a kit. While you wear light armor, you gain a +3 <a href="../../../../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties/">bonus</a> to <a href="../../../../rule/health/stamina/">Stamina</a>, and that <a href="../../../../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties/">bonus</a> increases by 3 at 4th, 7th, and 10th levels. While you wield a light weapon, you gain a +1 damage <a href="../../../../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties/">bonus</a> with weapon abilities, including <a href="../../../common/main-actions/free-strike/">free strikes</a>. You can use light armor treasures and light weapon treasures.</p>
<p>If you have a kit, you can&#39;t take this blessing.</p>
</div>
