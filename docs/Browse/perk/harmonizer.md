# Harmonizer

---

You can make a [Presence](../rule/character/presence.md) [test](../rule/test/test.md) using the Music skill to influence creatures who don't have emotions or can't understand you. Additionally, once during a negotiation when an ally makes an argument, you can play music to give that ally an [edge](../rule/dice/edge.md) on their [test](../rule/test/test.md).
