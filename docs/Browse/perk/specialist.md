# Specialist

---

You are a leading expert on a particular subject. Choose one skill you have from the lore skill group. You always have a double [edge](../rule/dice/edge.md) on tests made to recall lore using this skill. Additionally, your [specialist](specialist.md) knowledge grants you notoriety in fields related to the chosen skill. You treat your Renown as 1 higher when negotiating with an NPC who knows your reputation, or 2 higher if they have the same skill you chose for this perk.
