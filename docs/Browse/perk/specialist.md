# Specialist

---

You are a leading expert on a particular subject. Choose one skill you have from the [lore skill group](../skill/lore/index.md). You always have a double [edge](../rule/dice/edge.md) on [tests](../rule/test/test.md) made to recall lore using this skill. Additionally, your [specialist](specialist.md) knowledge grants you notoriety in fields related to the chosen skill. You treat your [Renown](../rule/resource/renown.md) as 1 higher when negotiating with an [NPC](../rule/general/npc.md) who knows your reputation, or 2 higher if they have the same skill you chose for this perk.
