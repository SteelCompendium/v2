# Spot the Tell

---

Whenever you make a [test](../rule/test/test.md) to read a person and obtain a tier 3 outcome, you notice several tells that give away their true feelings. Any [test](../rule/test/test.md) you make to read that person in the future gains an [edge](../rule/dice/edge.md).
