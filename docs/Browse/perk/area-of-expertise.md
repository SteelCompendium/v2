# Area of Expertise

---

Choose one skill you already have from the [crafting skill group](../skill/group/crafting.md). Whenever you obtain a tier 1 outcome on an easy or medium [test](../rule/test/test.md) using this skill, you treat it as a tier 2 outcome instead. Additionally, if you spend 1 minute inspecting an object related to the chosen skill, you can estimate its value and learn of any flaws in its construction.
