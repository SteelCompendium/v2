# But I Know Who Does

---

Whenever you fail a [test](../rule/test/test.md) to recall lore using a skill from the [lore skill group](../skill/lore/index.md), you instinctively recall the nearest location where the information you seek might be found. This could be the tower of a local sage, a library in a nearby city, somewhere deep in a dungeon, or any other location of the Director's determination. The Director can decide that certain lore can't be revealed this way.
