# Lost in Time

---

In a long-ago age, a cataclysm overtook your city. You weren't killed, but some arcane accident caused you to be suspended in time until now. Alone, you must navigate the world around you with a head full of outdated memories—and a few ancient secrets.

**Benefit:** Choose a [damage type](../rule/damage/damage-type.md) from acid, cold, corruption, fire, holy, lightning, poison, psychic, or sonic. Whenever you use a [signature ability](../rule/combat/signature-ability.md), you can have it deal your chosen [damage type](../rule/damage/damage-type.md) instead of its usual damage.

**Drawback:** You automatically fail any [test](../rule/test/test.md) made to recall information from the period during which you were suspended in time.
