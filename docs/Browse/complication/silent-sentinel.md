# Silent Sentinel

---

You were trained by a group of spies, who psionically infused silence into your every step and enhanced your ability to hear distant whispers. But your enhanced hearing has some nasty side effects.

**Benefit:** You have the Eavesdrop and Sneak skills from the [intrigue skill group](../skill/intrigue/index.md), plus one skill of your choice from the [lore skill group](../skill/lore/index.md). Additionally, you can telepathically communicate with any creature provided they share a language with you and you can observe each other. A creature you communicate with this way can respond telepathically if they choose.

**Drawback:** You have sonic weakness 5. Additionally, whenever you take sonic damage, you are [dazed](../condition/dazed.md) until the end of your next [turn](../rule/combat/turn.md).
