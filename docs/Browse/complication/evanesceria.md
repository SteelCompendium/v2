# Evanesceria

---

You have contracted a rare magical disease called [evanesceria](evanesceria.md). From time to time, you're not quite yourself—or anyone else either. You simply... vanish, then return later with no memory of your absence.

**Benefit:** At the start of any [combat round](../rule/combat/combat-round.md), you can attempt to absent yourself from reality by rolling a d10. On a 6 or higher, you disappear, then reappear in the space you left or the nearest unoccupied space of your choice when you take your [turn](../rule/combat/turn.md). You can't attempt to absent yourself again until you earn 1 or more [Victories](../rule/resource/victories.md).

**Drawback:** Whenever you start a [respite](../rule/resource/respite.md) activity, roll 2d10. If you roll a 1 on either die, you inadvertently absent yourself from reality, reappearing at the end of the [respite](../rule/resource/respite.md). You gain the benefits of taking a [respite](../rule/resource/respite.md) but don't perform the [respite](../rule/resource/respite.md) activity.
