# Sewer Folk

---

Impoverished or on the run, you spent your formative years living in the sewers of a major city. There, you learned lessons that have served you well, although the miasma of the sewers did permanent damage to your health.

**Benefit:** You can automatically climb or swim (your choice) at full [speed](../rule/character/speed.md) while moving, and you never get lost while underground. Additionally, while in a city with sewers, you and your companions can move from place to place without being detected, as the Director determines.

**Drawback:** You have poison weakness 5.
