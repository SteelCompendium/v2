# Grifter

---

You used to be a con artist, but those days are pretty much behind you. Being a hero is an even better racket. After all, if you're saving the world, who can be mad at you for stealing a few coins along the way?

**Benefit:** You have one skill of your choice from the [intrigue skill group](../skill/intrigue/index.md).

**Drawback:** Whenever you meet an [NPC](../rule/general/npc.md) for the first time, the Director can decide that [NPC](../rule/general/npc.md) was a victim of one of your previous cons and remembers you. If they do so, the party gains a [hero token](../rule/resource/hero-token.md).
