# Curse of Stone

---

As a child, you met a creature who turns people to stone, such as a medusa. You escaped half petrified, avoiding the fate of others who stand as statues now.

**Benefit:** You have a +1 bonus to stability. Additionally, you can use a free maneuver to cause your body, gear, and any items you hold to take on the appearance of stone, making you appear to be a [mundane](mundane.md) statue while you remain unmoving.

**Drawback:** You have sonic weakness 5. Additionally, while you are [winded](../rule/health/winded.md), you are [dazed](../condition/dazed.md).
