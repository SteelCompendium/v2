# Promising Apprentice

---

You were apprenticed to learn a crafting trade. Your mentor said you had a special gift and might well become a master of your craft someday. But before your training was complete, your mentor was killed.

**Benefit:** You have one skill of your choice from the [crafting skill group](../skill/crafting/index.md). Additionally, choose one of your skills from the [crafting group](../skill/crafting/index.md). You gain an [edge](../rule/dice/edge.md) on any [test](../rule/test/test.md) that uses that skill.

**Drawback:** Whoever killed your mentor cursed you. You take a [bane](../rule/dice/bane.md) on any [test](../rule/test/test.md) that doesn't use one of your skills.
