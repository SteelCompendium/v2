# Loner

---

You've always been a lone wolf. With no one else to lean on, you've picked up a million survival tricks. Which made it all the more surprising when you joined your current adventuring group and found the family you'd never known you needed.

**Benefit:** When you finish a [respite](../rule/resource/respite.md), choose a skill you don't have. You have that skill until the end of your next [respite](../rule/resource/respite.md).

**Drawback:** Now that you finally have people who care about you, you won't let anyone take them away! Whenever a creature reduces one of your allies to 0 [Stamina](../rule/health/stamina.md), you are [taunted](../condition/taunted.md) by that creature until your ally's [Stamina](../rule/health/stamina.md) is higher than 0, another creature makes you [taunted](../condition/taunted.md), or the end of the encounter.
