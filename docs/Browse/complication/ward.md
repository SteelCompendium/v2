# Ward

---

Your childhood sweetheart was royalty, and the two of you stayed close throughout the years. When your former sweetheart died, you swore an oath to dedicate your life to become a role model for their child, advising them in the ways of being a benevolent monarch.

**Benefit:** You know how to talk to monarchs, aristocrats, and other wealthy leaders. When you engage with any such [NPC](../rule/general/npc.md) during a negotiation, their patience increases by 1 (to a maximum of 5).

**Drawback:** Your royal [ward](ward.md) can be a burden. Whenever you take a [respite](../rule/resource/respite.md), roll a d10. On a 1, your [ward](ward.md) contacts you and requires your help during the [respite](../rule/resource/respite.md), requiring you to spend your time helping them instead of undertaking a [respite](../rule/resource/respite.md) activity.
