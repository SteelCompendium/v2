# Wrongly Imprisoned

---

You spent many years imprisoned for a crime you didn't commit. During your long hours of solitary confinement, you honed your skills and recited the names of those who framed you. Someday, you will have your revenge.

**Benefit:** You have two skills of your choice, neither of which can be from the [interpersonal skill group](../skill/interpersonal/index.md).

**Drawback:** Your health suffered in prison. Whenever you are [winded](../rule/health/winded.md), you are stricken with a hacking cough that makes it impossible for you to hide or sneak.
