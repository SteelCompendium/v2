# Animal Form

---

Due to a magical accident, your being has fused with that of a small, harmless animal. You turn into this animal when it's convenient—and sometimes when it's inconvenient as well.

**Benefit:** As a maneuver, you take the form of a specific animal of [size](../rule/character/size.md) 1T. You retain all your other statistics aside from your [size](../rule/character/size.md), but you can't talk or use actions, and the only maneuvers you can use are [Escape Grab](../feature/common/maneuvers/escape-grab.md), Hide, and Stand Up. Based on the animal you can turn into, you might be able to [burrow](../movement/burrow.md) or [fly](../movement/fly.md), or to automatically climb or swim at full [speed](../rule/character/speed.md) while moving. If your [animal form](animal-form.md) doesn't provide such additional movement, you have a +2 [bonus](../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md) to [speed](../rule/character/speed.md).

Unless you use this benefit again, you return to your true form at the start of your next [turn](../rule/combat/turn.md).

**Drawback:** At the start of any [turn](../rule/combat/turn.md) while you are [winded](../rule/health/winded.md), the Director can spend 1 Malice to force you to take your [animal form](animal-form.md). Once the Director has done so, they can't do so again until you have finished a [respite](../rule/resource/respite.md).
