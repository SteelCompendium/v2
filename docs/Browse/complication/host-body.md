# Host Body

---

"Do not be alarmed! We are not the humanoid we appear to be. We are an intelligent fungal collective, using this body as a host. No, we are doing nothing unsavory! This body was dead when we found it, and we merely gave it another chance at life. We are friendly. Please put down those torches!"

**Benefit:** You are a sapient fungus who inhabits a humanoid body. Your [host body](host-body.md) follows all the usual rules for a character and is considered to be alive. At any time while your [host body](host-body.md) is alive, or for 24 hours after it dies, you can use a main action to move to a dead humanoid within 10 squares of the body and use it as your new [host body](host-body.md), provided the body belongs to a playable ancestry. When you do so, your original [host body](host-body.md) dies if it was alive. Your new [host body](host-body.md) gains all your statistics except [size](../rule/character/size.md), [ancestry traits](../../Read/heroes/ancestries.md), and other statistics related to your former [host body's](host-body.md) ancestry, which you instead gain from your new [host body](host-body.md). When you inhabit a new [host body](host-body.md), you start with 1 [Stamina](../rule/health/stamina.md) and can immediately spend a [Recovery](../rule/health/recoveries.md).

**Drawback:** You have fire weakness 5. Additionally, you take a [bane](../rule/dice/bane.md) on any [test](../rule/test/test.md) made to read a humanoid creature's emotions or body language.
