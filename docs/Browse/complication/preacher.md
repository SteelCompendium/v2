# Preacher

---

When you were young, you almost died in an accident or attack, but a vision of a god or [saint](../rule/world/saint.md) showed you the way to save yourself and others you loved. That event drove you into the church and gave you a strong belief in a particular religion or cause—and you can't wait to tell other people all about it.

**Benefit:** As a [respite](../rule/resource/respite.md) activity, you can attempt to convert members of a community to your cause. Make a [Presence](../rule/character/presence.md) [test](../rule/test/test.md) with a difficulty determined by the Director based on the community's receptiveness to your ideas. On a success, you convert one [NPC](../rule/general/npc.md) into a [follower](../rule/general/follower.md) (see [Renown](../rule/resource/renown.md) in Chapter 13: [Rewards](../../Read/heroes/rewards.md)), which you gain in addition to any [followers](../rule/general/follower.md) acquired through [Renown](../rule/resource/renown.md) or other means. The Director determines the type of [follower](../rule/general/follower.md). Once you have converted an [NPC](../rule/general/npc.md) into a [follower](../rule/general/follower.md) this way, you can't use this benefit again until you gain a level.

**Drawback:** If you fail in your conversion attempt, one of your existing [followers](../rule/general/follower.md) of the Director's choice (whether gained through this complication or your [Renown](../rule/resource/renown.md) or other means) leaves you, their faith in you shaken. If you have no [followers](../rule/general/follower.md), your [Renown](../rule/resource/renown.md) is reduced by 1. If you need to reduce your [Renown](../rule/resource/renown.md) and it's already 0, you gain no benefits from the [respite](../rule/resource/respite.md) during which you make the conversion attempt.
