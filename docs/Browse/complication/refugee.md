# Refugee

---

A hostile army—perhaps the forces of Ajax, the Iron [Saint](../rule/world/saint.md)—conquered your homeland. Your family escaped, but you can't return home until your oppressors are defeated once and for all.

**Benefit:** When your family fled your homeland, they left their most valuable asset behind. Work with the Director to determine whether this asset is a [trinket](../rule/treasure/trinket.md) or [leveled treasure](../rule/treasure/leveled-treasure.md), several points of [Wealth](../rule/resource/wealth.md), the [project source](../rule/downtime/project-source.md) to create a treasure, or the like. This asset is in the hands of the invaders but can be won back as the Director determines.

**Drawback:** The faction that invaded your homeland wants you captured or dead. Any of their agents or sympathizers attempt to harm you if they recognize you, as the Director determines.
