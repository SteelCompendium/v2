# Self-Taught

---

While your peers were learning their trades in fancy schools, you honed your capabilities on the mean streets with nothing but your own instinct as a [guide](../rule/downtime/guide.md). What you lost in polish and tactical acumen, you now make up for in raw power.

**Benefit and Drawback:** At the start of each of your [turns](../rule/combat/turn.md) during combat, you can forgo gaining your [Heroic Resource](../rule/resource/heroic-resource.md) until the start of your next [turn](../rule/combat/turn.md). If you do, your [strikes](../rule/combat/strike.md) gain a damage [bonus](../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md) equal to your highest [characteristic](../rule/character/characteristic.md) score until the start of your next [turn](../rule/combat/turn.md).
