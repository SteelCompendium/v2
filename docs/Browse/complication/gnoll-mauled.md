# Gnoll-Mauled

---

As a child, you survived a gnoll attack. But that attack left you with a jagged scar and the occasional fit of bloodlust.

**Benefit:** Whenever an ally within 5 squares is reduced to 0 [Stamina](../rule/health/stamina.md), you can use a [triggered action](../rule/combat/triggered-action.md) to move up to your [speed](../rule/character/speed.md) and make a [free strike](../feature/common/main-actions/free-strike.md).

**Drawback:** While you are [dazed](../condition/dazed.md), if you start your [turn](../rule/combat/turn.md) [adjacent](../rule/combat/adjacent.md) to one or more creatures, you must use your main action to make a [melee](../rule/combat/melee.md) [free strike](../feature/common/main-actions/free-strike.md) against an [adjacent](../rule/combat/adjacent.md) creature.

**Special:** You can't take this complication if you can't be made [dazed](../condition/dazed.md).
