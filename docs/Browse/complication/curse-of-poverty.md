# Curse of Poverty

---

A soothsayer once predicted you would have a long life, even as they told you you'd never be rich. But you're determined to prove them wrong. You'll get rich or die trying!

**Benefit and Drawback:** Whenever you take a [respite](../rule/resource/respite.md) while your [Wealth](../rule/resource/wealth.md) is higher than 1, some improbable event occurs that causes most of your money to vanish—including money you've hidden, loaned to others, or given away. Your [Wealth](../rule/resource/wealth.md) is reduced to 1. For each point of [Wealth](../rule/resource/wealth.md) you lose this way, your number of [Recoveries](../rule/health/recoveries.md) increases by 1. Your [Recoveries](../rule/health/recoveries.md) reset to their usual value the first time you take a [respite](../rule/resource/respite.md) with fewer [Recoveries](../rule/health/recoveries.md) than your maximum.
