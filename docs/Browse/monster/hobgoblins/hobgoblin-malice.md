# Hobgoblin Malice

---

At the start of any hobgoblin's turn, you can spend [Malice](../../rule/monster/malice.md) to activate one of the following features.

> ⭐️ **Goblin Malice Features (3-7 [Malice](../../rule/monster/malice.md))**
>
> The hobgoblin activates a [Malice](../../rule/monster/malice.md) Feature available to goblins.

> ⭐️ **Operation Goblin Mode (3 [Malice](../../rule/monster/malice.md))**
>
> Each goblin in the encounter gains a +3 bonus to speed until the end of the round.

> ⭐️ **Operation Tactical Swarm (5 [Malice](../../rule/monster/malice.md))**
>
> Each hobgoblin in the encounter [shifts](../../movement/shifting.md) up to their speed and can take the Defend main action.

> 🌀 **Operation Earth Sear (7 [Malice](../../rule/monster/malice.md))**
>
> Until the end of the round, the ground throughout the encounter map becomes blazing hot. Any enemy takes 1 fire damage for each square of the ground they enter. Any enemy who ends their turn on the ground has fire weakness 2 until the start of their next turn.
