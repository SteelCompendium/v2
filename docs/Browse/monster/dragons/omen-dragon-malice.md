# Omen Dragon Malice

---

At the start of an omen dragon's turn, you can spend [Malice](../../rule/monster/malice.md) to activate one of the following features.

> ⭐️ **Black Skies (3 [Malice](../../rule/monster/malice.md))**
>
> The dragon expands their wings to create a shroud of shadow. Until the start of the dragon's next turn, any strike made against them takes a [bane](../../rule/dice/bane.md).

> ❇️ **Rise and Fall (5 [Malice](../../rule/monster/malice.md))**
>
> The dragon [flies](../../movement/fly.md) up to 10 squares and carries fated souls with them. Each creature in the area of the dragon's Stagnant Wyrmscale Aura [trait](../../rule/monster/monster-trait.md) makes a **Presence test**.
>
> - **≤11:** Vertical pull 10
> - **12-16:** Vertical pull 6
> - **17+:** Vertical pull 4

> ☠️ **Solo Action (5 [Malice](../../rule/monster/malice.md))**
>
> The dragon takes an additional main action on their turn. They can use this feature even if they are [dazed](../../condition/dazed.md).

> 🌀 **Burn It Right Down (10 [Malice](../../rule/monster/malice.md))**
>
> Each edge of the encounter map burns with intangible purple flames until the end of the encounter. The flames expand by 1 square at the end of every turn. Any enemy takes 5 corruption damage for each square of flames they enter.
