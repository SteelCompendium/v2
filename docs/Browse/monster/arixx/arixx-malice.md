# Arixx Malice

---

At the start of an arixx's turn, you can spend Malice to activate one of the following features.

> 👤 **Burning Maw (3 [Malice](../../rule/monster/malice.md))**
>
> The arixx dribbles acid over their mandibles, causing the next strike they make to gain an [edge](../../rule/dice/edge.md) and deal an extra 3 acid damage.

> 🔳 **Geyser (5 [Malice](../../rule/monster/malice.md))**
>
> The arixx's underground tunnels swell with pressure, causing a sudden influx of hot gas to burst from a 3-square-by-3-square area anywhere on the surface. Each enemy in the area makes an **[Agility](../../rule/character/agility.md) test**.
>
> - **≤11:** 4 damage; vertical [push](../../movement/forced-movement.md) 5
> - **12-16:** 4 damage; vertical [push](../../movement/forced-movement.md) 3
> - **17+:** The target [shifts](../../movement/shifting.md) to the nearest unoccupied space outside the area.

> ☠️ **Solo Action (5 [Malice](../../rule/monster/malice.md))**
>
> The arixx takes an additional main action on their turn. They can use this feature even if they are [dazed](../../condition/dazed.md).

> 🌀 **Earth Sink (7 [Malice](../../rule/monster/malice.md))**
>
> The encounter map suddenly quakes, then begins to sink. Each creature on the ground who has A < 1 is knocked [prone](../../condition/prone.md). Until the end of the encounter, each creature who starts their turn on the ground and can't [burrow](../../movement/burrow.md) must spend 1 additional square of movement to leave their starting position, or 2 squares if they start their turn [prone](../../condition/prone.md) or underground. A creature who starts and ends their turn in the same space on the ground and can't [burrow](../../movement/burrow.md) sinks 1 square into the ground.
