# Demon Malice (Level 1+ Malice Features)

---

At the start of any demon's turn, you can spend [Malice](../../../rule/monster/malice.md) to activate one of the following features.

> ⭐️ **Soulburn (3 [Malice](../../../rule/monster/malice.md))**
>
> Each demon acting this turn has a double [edge](../../../rule/dice/edge.md) on abilities.

> 🌀 **Abyssal Evolution (7 [Malice](../../../rule/monster/malice.md))**
>
> A demon [minion](../../../rule/organization/minion.md) of your choice transforms into a non-minion [horde](../../../rule/organization/horde.md) demon of the same level.

> 🔳 **Abyssal Rift (7 [Malice](../../../rule/monster/malice.md))**
>
> Two size 2 rifts to the Abyssal Wasteland appear at locations of your choice. Any demon can use an abyssal rift as a portal to another abyssal rift in the encounter, moving into any space in one rift and appearing immediately in any unoccupied space in the other rift. A non-demon who enters a rift for the first time in a round or starts their turn there takes corruption damage equal to the level of the highest-level demon on the encounter map. An abyssal rift is an immovable object that has 25 [Stamina](../../../rule/health/stamina.md), [damage immunity](../../../rule/damage/damage-immunity.md) 2, and holy [weakness](../../../rule/damage/damage-weakness.md) 5. The rift closes when there are no demons remaining on the encounter map. Additionally, a creature who has the Magic or Psionics skill can make a Reason test or Intuition test as a maneuver while [adjacent](../../../rule/combat/adjacent.md) to a rift to destabilize and close it.
>
> - **≤11:** The rift remains open and regains 5 [Stamina](../../../rule/health/stamina.md).
> - **12-16:** The rift remains open.
> - **17+:** The rift closes.
