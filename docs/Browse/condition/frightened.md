# Frightened

---

When a creature is [frightened](frightened.md), any ability roll they make against the source of their fear takes a [bane](../rule/dice/bane.md). If that source is a creature, their ability rolls made against the [frightened](frightened.md) creature gain an [edge](../rule/dice/edge.md). A [frightened](frightened.md) creature can't willingly move closer to the source of their fear if they know the location of that source. If a creature gains the [frightened](frightened.md) condition from one source while already [frightened](frightened.md) by a different source, the new condition replaces the old one.
