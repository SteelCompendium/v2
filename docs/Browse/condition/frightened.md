# Frightened

---

When a creature is [frightened](frightened.md), any [ability roll](../rule/dice/ability-roll.md) they make against the source of their fear takes a [bane](../rule/dice/bane.md). If that source is a creature, their [ability rolls](../rule/dice/ability-roll.md) made against the [frightened](frightened.md) creature gain an [edge](../rule/dice/edge.md). A [frightened](frightened.md) creature can't willingly move closer to the source of their fear if they know the location of that source. If a creature gains the [frightened](frightened.md) [condition](../rule/combat/condition.md) from one source while already [frightened](frightened.md) by a different source, the new [condition](../rule/combat/condition.md) replaces the old one.
