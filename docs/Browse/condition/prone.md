# Prone

---

While a creature is [prone](prone.md), they are flat on the ground, any strike they make takes a [bane](../rule/dice/bane.md), and [melee](../rule/combat/melee.md) abilities used against them gain an [edge](../rule/dice/edge.md). A [prone](prone.md) creature must [crawl](../movement/crawl.md) to move along the ground, which costs 1 additional square of movement for every square crawled. A creature can't climb, [jump](../movement/jump.md), swim, or [fly](../movement/fly.md) while [prone](prone.md). If they are climbing, [flying](../movement/fly.md), or jumping when knocked [prone](prone.md), they fall.

Unless the ability or effect that imposed the [prone](prone.md) [condition](../rule/combat/condition.md) says otherwise, a [prone](prone.md) creature can stand up using the [Stand Up](../feature/common/maneuvers/stand-up.md) maneuver (see Maneuvers in Chapter 10: [Combat](../../Read/heroes/combat.md)). A creature [adjacent](../rule/combat/adjacent.md) to a willing [prone](prone.md) creature can likewise use the [Stand Up](../feature/common/maneuvers/stand-up.md) maneuver to make that creature stand up.
