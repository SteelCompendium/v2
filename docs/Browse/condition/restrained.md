# Restrained

A creature who is [restrained](restrained.md) has speed 0, can't use the Stand Up maneuver, and can't be [force moved](../movement/forced-movement.md). A [restrained](restrained.md) creature takes a bane on ability rolls and on Might and Agility tests, and abilities used against them gain an edge.

If a creature [teleports](../movement/teleport.md) while [restrained](restrained.md), that condition ends.
