# Restrained

---

A creature who is [restrained](restrained.md) has [speed](../rule/character/speed.md) 0, can't use the [Stand Up](../feature/common/maneuvers/stand-up.md) maneuver, and can't be [force moved](../movement/forced-movement.md). A [restrained](restrained.md) creature takes a [bane](../rule/dice/bane.md) on [ability rolls](../rule/dice/ability-roll.md) and on [Might](../rule/character/might.md) and [Agility](../rule/character/agility.md) [tests](../rule/test/test.md), and abilities used against them gain an [edge](../rule/dice/edge.md).

If a creature [teleports](../movement/teleport.md) while [restrained](restrained.md), that [condition](../rule/combat/condition.md) ends.
