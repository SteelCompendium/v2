# Bleeding

---

While a creature is [bleeding](bleeding.md), whenever they use a main action, use a [triggered action](../rule/combat/triggered-action.md), or make a [test](../rule/test/test.md) or [ability roll](../rule/dice/ability-roll.md) using [Might](../rule/character/might.md) or [Agility](../rule/character/agility.md), they lose [Stamina](../rule/health/stamina.md) equal to 1d6 + their level after the main action, [triggered action](../rule/combat/triggered-action.md), or [power roll](../rule/dice/power-roll.md) is resolved. This [Stamina](../rule/health/stamina.md) loss can't be prevented in any way, and only happens once per action.

You take damage from this [condition](../rule/combat/condition.md) when you use a main action off your [turn](../rule/combat/turn.md). For example, a [signature ability](../rule/combat/signature-ability.md) used as a free [triggered action](../rule/combat/triggered-action.md) with the assistance of the [tactician's](../class/tactician.md) [Strike Now](../feature/ability/tactician/level-1/strike-now.md) ability triggers the damage from the [bleeding](bleeding.md) [condition](../rule/combat/condition.md).
