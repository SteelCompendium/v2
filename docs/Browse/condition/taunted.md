# Taunted

---

A creature who is [taunted](taunted.md) has a double [bane](../rule/dice/bane.md) on [ability rolls](../rule/dice/ability-roll.md) for any ability that doesn't target the creature who [taunted](taunted.md) them, as long as they have [line of effect](../rule/combat/line-of-effect.md) to that creature. If a creature gains the [taunted](taunted.md) [condition](../rule/combat/condition.md) from one source while already [taunted](taunted.md) by a different source, the new [condition](../rule/combat/condition.md) replaces the old one.
