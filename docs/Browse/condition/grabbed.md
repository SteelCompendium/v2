# Grabbed

---

A creature who is [grabbed](grabbed.md) has speed 0, can't be [force moved](../movement/forced-movement.md) except by a creature, object, or effect that has them [grabbed](grabbed.md), can't use the Knockback maneuver (see Maneuvers in Chapter 10: [Combat](../../Read/heroes/combat.md)), and takes a bane on abilities that don't target the creature, object, or effect that has them [grabbed](grabbed.md). If a creature is [grabbed](grabbed.md) by another creature and that creature moves, they bring the [grabbed](grabbed.md) creature with them. If a creature's size is equal to or less than the size of a creature they have [grabbed](grabbed.md), their speed is halved while they have that creature [grabbed](grabbed.md).

A creature who has another creature [grabbed](grabbed.md) can use a maneuver to move the [grabbed](grabbed.md) creature into an unoccupied space adjacent to them.

A creature can release a creature they have [grabbed](grabbed.md) at any time to end that condition (no action required). A [grabbed](grabbed.md) creature can attempt to escape being [grabbed](grabbed.md) using the Escape Grab maneuver (see Chapter 10: [Combat](../../Read/heroes/combat.md)). If a [grabbed](grabbed.md) creature [teleports](../movement/teleport.md), or if either the [grabbed](grabbed.md) creature or the creature grabbing them is [force moved](../movement/forced-movement.md) so that both creatures are not adjacent to each other, that creature is no longer [grabbed](grabbed.md).

A creature can grab only creatures of their size or smaller. If a creature's Might score is 2 or higher, they can grab any creature larger than them with a size equal to or less than their Might score.

Unless otherwise indicated, a creature can grab only one creature at a time.
