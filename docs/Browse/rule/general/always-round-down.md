# Always Round Down

---

Sometimes the rules tell you to divide a number in half. Whenever you divide an odd number in half and it results in a decimal, round the result down to the nearest whole number. For instance, if a [tactician](../../class/tactician.md) takes 7 damage and uses the Parry ability in response—a [triggered action](../combat/triggered-action.md) that halves the damage—then the damage is reduced to 3.
