# Test Difficulty

---

The Director decides how difficult a task that requires a [test](test.md) is: easy, medium, or hard. If a task seems as though it's easier than easy, then no [test](test.md) is necessary. The hero simply accomplishes the task. If the task seems harder than hard, then the Director is free to decide that it's impossible to complete with a [test](test.md).

On a [test](test.md)-by-[test](test.md) basis, the Director can share the difficulty of a task before the player makes the [test](test.md), which makes interpreting the outcome faster at the table. The Director can also keep a [test](test.md)'s difficulty secret until after the player rolls the [test](test.md), for dramatic effect.

The [Test](test.md) Difficulty Outcomes table shows all the possible outcomes of the different difficulties of [tests](test.md). The Director will keep this information handy so as to be able to compare the different difficulties and their outcomes during play.

### Test Difficulty Outcomes Table

| [Power Roll](../dice/power-roll.md)       | Easy [Test](test.md) Outcomes         | Medium [Test](test.md) Outcomes       | Hard [Test](test.md) Outcomes         |
|------------------|----------------------------|----------------------------|----------------------------|
| ≤11              | Success with a consequence | Failure                    | Failure with a consequence |
| 12-16            | Success                    | Success with a consequence | Failure                    |
| 17+              | Success with a reward      | Success                    | Success                    |
| [Natural 19 or 20](../dice/natural-19-20.md) | Success with a reward      | Success with a reward      | Success with a reward      |

Whenever the rules talk about obtaining a success on a [test](test.md), that includes a straight success, a success with a consequence, or a success with a reward. Whenever the rules talk about a failure on a [test](test.md), that includes a straight failure or a failure with a consequence.

Whenever you make a [test](test.md) whose outcome you don't like, you can spend a [hero token](../resource/hero-token.md) to reroll the [test](test.md). You must use the new roll.

## Easy Tests

An easy [test](test.md) has some risk of consequence, but most heroes will likely overcome it. The [power roll](../dice/power-roll.md) you make for an easy [test](test.md) determines the outcome (see [Test](test.md) Outcomes below):

- **≤11:** You succeed on the task and incur a consequence.
- **12-16:** You succeed on the task.
- **17+:** You succeed on the task with a reward.

## Medium Tests

A medium [test](test.md) has some risk of failure that most heroes will likely overcome—but with a cost. The [power roll](../dice/power-roll.md) you make for a medium [test](test.md) determines the outcome:

- **≤11:** You fail the task.
- **12-16:** You succeed on the task and incur a consequence.
- **17+:** You succeed on the task.

## Hard Tests

A hard [test](test.md) has a greater risk of failure, and most heroes are likely to suffer some hardship while trying to overcome the intended task. The [power roll](../dice/power-roll.md) you make for a hard [test](test.md) determines the outcome:

- **≤11:** You fail the task and incur a consequence.
- **12-16:** You fail the task.
- **17+:** You succeed on the task.

## Natural 19 or 20: Success With a Reward {data-scc="mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/natural-19-20"}

Whenever you get a natural 19 or 20 on the [power roll](../dice/power-roll.md) for a [test](test.md)—a total of 19 or 20 before adding your [characteristic](../character/characteristic.md) score or other modifiers you score a critical success. This critical success automatically lets you succeed on the task with a reward, even if the [test](test.md) has a medium or hard difficulty.
