# Assist a Test

---

You can attempt to assist another creature with a [test](test.md) they make, provided you have a skill that applies to the [test](test.md), the other creature isn't using that same skill on the [test](test.md), and you can describe how your character helps to the Director's satisfaction. In other words, your attempt to help has to make sense, and you have to bring some useful expertise to the table. Helping another creature sneak by shouting encouragement at them isn't going to make them stealthier.

When you attempt to assist another creature, make a [test](test.md) using the skill you choose, and using a [characteristic](../character/characteristic.md) chosen by the Director based on the activity you use to help. The outcome of that [test](test.md) determines the [bonus](../dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md) applied to the [test](test.md) you're assisting:

- **≤11:** You get in the way or make things worse. The creature takes a [bane](../dice/bane.md) on their [test](test.md).
- **12-16:** Your help grants the other creature an [edge](../dice/edge.md) on their [test](test.md).
- **17+:** Your help gives the other creature a double [edge](../dice/edge.md) on their [test](test.md).

For example, when an ally tries to pick a jailer's pocket, you might attempt to assist by using the [Flirt](../../skill/interpersonal/flirt.md) skill to distract the jailer. The Director accepts this, and asks you to make a [Presence](../character/presence.md) [test](test.md) using Flirt. The outcome of that [test](test.md) determines the [bonus](../dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md) you provide to the other hero's [Agility](../character/agility.md) [test](test.md) to pick the jailer's pocket—or whether you fumble the distraction and potentially draw attention to the attempt.
