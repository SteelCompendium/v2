# Concealment

---

Darkness, fog, invisibility magic, and any other effect that fully obscures a creature or object but doesn't protect their physical form grants that creature or object concealment. Even if you have [line of effect](line-of-effect.md) to such a target, a creature or object has concealment from you if you can't see or otherwise observe them. You can target a creature or object with concealment using a [strike](strike.md), provided they aren't hidden (see Hide  and Sneak in Chapter 9: [Tests](../../../Read/heroes/tests.md)). However, [strikes](strike.md) against such targets take a [bane](../dice/bane.md).

## Invisible Creatures

Invisible creatures always have [concealment](concealment.md) from other creatures. If an invisible creature isn't hidden, they can still be targeted by abilities. The [test](../test/test.md) made to find a hidden creature who is invisible takes a [bane](../dice/bane.md).
