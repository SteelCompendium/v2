# End of Next Turn (EoT)

---

Many effects last until the end of the target's next [turn](turn.md), abbreviated as "(EoT)" in the [tier outcomes](../dice/tier-outcome.md) for an ability's [power roll](../dice/power-roll.md). A creature suffers from such an effect until the end of their next [turn](turn.md), or the end of their current [turn](turn.md) if the effect was imposed on their current [turn](turn.md).
