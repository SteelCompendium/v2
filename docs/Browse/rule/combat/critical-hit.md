# Critical Hit

---

Whenever you make an [ability roll](../dice/ability-roll.md) as a main action and the roll is a [natural 19](../dice/natural-19-20.md) or [natural 20](../dice/natural-19-20.md)-a total of 19 or 20 before adding your [characteristic](../character/characteristic.md) score or other modifiers—you score a critical hit. A critical hit allows you to immediately take an additional main action after resolving the [power roll](../dice/power-roll.md), whether or not it's your [turn](turn.md) and even if you are [dazed](../../condition/dazed.md) (see [Conditions](condition.md) below).

You can't score a critical hit with an [ability roll](../dice/ability-roll.md) made as a maneuver or any other action type, but you can score a critical hit with a main action you use off your [turn](turn.md). For example, an [opportunity attack](opportunity-attack.md) made as a [triggered action](triggered-action.md) or a [signature ability](signature-ability.md) used as a free [triggered action](triggered-action.md) with the assistance of the [tactician's](../../class/tactician.md) [Strike Now](../../feature/ability/tactician/level-1/strike-now.md) ability can be critical hits.
