# Underwater Combat

---

If a creature is fully submerged in water, they have fire immunity 5 and lightning weakness 5. If they can't automatically swim at full [speed](../character/speed.md) while moving, their [power rolls](../dice/power-roll.md) take a [bane](../dice/bane.md).
