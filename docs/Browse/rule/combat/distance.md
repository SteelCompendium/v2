# Distance

---

An ability's "Distance" entry, represented by this symbol 📏, indicates how close you need to be to a creature or object to affect that target with the ability.

## Melee

[Melee](melee.md) abilities have a [distance](distance.md) of "[Melee](melee.md) X" and require you to make contact with a creature with your body, a weapon, or an [implement](../treasure/implement.md). The number X is the maximum [distance](distance.md) in squares at which you can physically make contact with another creature or object targeted by the ability. For instance, a [distance](distance.md) of "[Melee](melee.md) 2" can be used to target creatures or objects within 2 squares of you, while "[Melee](melee.md) 1" limits you to [adjacent](adjacent.md) targets (those within 1 square).

## Ranged

[Ranged](ranged.md) abilities have a [distance](distance.md) of "[Ranged](ranged.md) X" and can be used to target creatures or objects too far away for you to make contact with. The number X is the maximum [distance](distance.md) in squares at which a creature or object can be targeted by the ability. For instance, a [distance](distance.md) of "[Ranged](ranged.md) 5" can be used to target creatures or objects within 5 squares of you.

If you make a [ranged](ranged.md) [strike](strike.md) while any enemy is [adjacent](adjacent.md) to you (within 1 square), you have a [bane](../dice/bane.md) on the [strike](strike.md)'s [power roll](../dice/power-roll.md). (See [Edges](../dice/edge.md) and [Banes](../dice/bane.md) in Chapter 1: [The Basics](../../../Read/heroes/the-basics.md).)

## Melee or Ranged

Some abilities have a [melee](melee.md) [distance](distance.md) and a [ranged](ranged.md) [distance](distance.md). When you use such an ability, you choose whether to use it as a [melee](melee.md) or a [ranged](ranged.md) ability.

An ability never has both the [Melee](melee.md) and [Ranged](ranged.md) keywords at the same time. For example, if you have the [Cloak and Dagger](../../kit/cloak-and-dagger.md) kit, which has a weapon damage [bonus](../dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md) to [melee](melee.md) abilities and a weapon damage [bonus](../dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md) to [ranged](ranged.md) abilities, only one [bonus](../dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md) at a time applies to an ability with both the [Melee](melee.md) and [Ranged](ranged.md) keywords. (See Chapter 6: [Kits](../../../Read/heroes/kits.md).)

## Self

If an ability has a [distance](distance.md) of "Self," that ability originates from you, and often affects only you. The ability's description specifies how it works.
