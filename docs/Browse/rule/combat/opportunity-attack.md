# Opportunity Attacks

---

Whenever a creature has an enemy [adjacent](adjacent.md) to them and the enemy willingly moves to a space that isn't [adjacent](adjacent.md) to the creature without [shifting](../../movement/shifting.md), the creature can take advantage of that movement to quickly make a [melee](melee.md) [free strike](../../feature/common/main-actions/free-strike.md) against the enemy as a free [triggered action](triggered-action.md). This is called an opportunity attack.

If a creature has a [bane](../dice/bane.md) or double [bane](../dice/bane.md) on the [power roll](../dice/power-roll.md) against the enemy, they can't make an opportunity attack.
