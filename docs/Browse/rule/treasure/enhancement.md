# Imbue Armor

---

<p>Armor imbued with an enhancement grants you special benefits while it is worn. Additionally, when your armor receives its 1st-level enhancement, it also grants a +6 <a href="../dice/bonuses-and-penalties/">bonus</a> to <a href="../health/stamina/">Stamina</a>. A 5th-level enhancement increases the <a href="../health/stamina/">Stamina</a> <a href="../dice/bonuses-and-penalties/">bonus</a> to +12, and a 9th-level enhancement increases it to +21.</p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Clothing as Armor Treasures</strong></p>
<p>A hero who has a kit that provides no armor, such as the <a href="../../kit/martial-artist/">Martial Artist</a> or <a href="../../kit/sniper/">Sniper</a> kit, can create and make use of leveled armor treasures that imbue mundane clothing with <a href="../general/supernatural/">supernatural</a> power. At the Director's discretion, heroes who don't use kits, such as <a href="../../class/elementalist/">elementalists</a> and <a href="../../class/null/">nulls</a>, can also benefit from such treasures.</p>
</blockquote>

<section class="pj__item"><div class="pj__item-head"><span class="dia" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="pj__item-name">Awe</span></div>
<div class="pj__item-body">
<p>When you start this project, choose either Charming or Threatening. If you choose Charming, you gain an <a href="../dice/edge/">edge</a> on <a href="../character/presence/">Presence</a> <a href="../test/test/">tests</a> made to win other creatures over or make a good impression. If you choose Threatening, you gain an <a href="../dice/edge/">edge</a> on <a href="../character/presence/">Presence</a> <a href="../test/test/">tests</a> made to intimidate, coerce, or bully.</p>
</div></section>
<section class="pj__item"><div class="pj__item-head"><span class="dia" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="pj__item-name"><a href="../../damage/damage-immunity/">Damage Immunity</a> I</span></div>
<div class="pj__item-body">
<p>When you start this project, select three <a href="../damage/damage-type/">damage types</a>. You have immunity 5 to those <a href="../damage/damage-type/">damage types</a>.</p>
</div></section>
<section class="pj__item"><div class="pj__item-head"><span class="dia" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="pj__item-name">Disguise</span></div>
<div class="pj__item-body">
<p>You can use a maneuver to cause this armor to take the form of any type of clothing that you have been in the presence of—a noble's dress, a guard's uniform, a cultist's robes, and so forth. The armor loses none of its protective qualities while transformed into other clothing.</p>
</div></section>
<section class="pj__item"><div class="pj__item-head"><span class="dia" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="pj__item-name">Iridescent</span></div>
<div class="pj__item-body">
<p>When you are the sole target of an ability, you can use a free <a href="../combat/triggered-action/">triggered action</a> to reveal that the ability was targeting an afterimage of you in the same space as you. The <a href="../dice/power-roll/">power roll</a> for the ability is treated as an 11. You can't use this <a href="enhancement/">enhancement</a> again until you earn 1 or more <a href="../resource/victories/">Victories</a>.</p>
</div></section>
<section class="pj__item"><div class="pj__item-head"><span class="dia" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="pj__item-name">Magic Resistance I</span></div>
<div class="pj__item-body">
<p>Your <a href="../character/characteristic/">characteristic</a> scores are treated as 1 higher (to a maximum of 2) for the purpose of resisting the <a href="../character/potency/">potencies</a> of magic abilities.</p>
</div></section>
<section class="pj__item"><div class="pj__item-head"><span class="dia" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="pj__item-name">Nettlebloom</span></div>
<div class="pj__item-body">
<p>Whenever you are <a href="../../condition/grabbed/">grabbed</a> by an <a href="../combat/adjacent/">adjacent</a> creature, your armor sprouts toxic nettles. While that creature has you <a href="../../condition/grabbed/">grabbed</a>, they are <a href="../../condition/weakened/">weakened</a>.</p>
</div></section>
<section class="pj__item"><div class="pj__item-head"><span class="dia" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="pj__item-name">Phasing I</span></div>
<div class="pj__item-body">
<p>Once per <a href="../combat/turn/">turn</a>, you can move through 1 square of solid matter. If you end your <a href="../combat/turn/">turn</a> inside solid matter, you are forced out into the space from which you entered it and you take 5 damage that can't be reduced in any way.</p>
</div></section>
<section class="pj__item"><div class="pj__item-head"><span class="dia" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="pj__item-name">Psionic Resistance I</span></div>
<div class="pj__item-body">
<p>Your <a href="../character/characteristic/">characteristic</a> scores are treated as 1 higher (to a maximum of 2) for the purpose of resisting the <a href="../character/potency/">potencies</a> of psionic abilities.</p>
</div></section>
<section class="pj__item"><div class="pj__item-head"><span class="dia" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="pj__item-name">Swift</span></div>
<div class="pj__item-body">
<p>You gain a +1 <a href="../dice/bonuses-and-penalties/">bonus</a> to <a href="../character/speed/">speed</a>.</p>
</div></section>
<section class="pj__item"><div class="pj__item-head"><span class="dia" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="pj__item-name">Tempest I</span></div>
<div class="pj__item-body">
<p>As a maneuver, you infuse this armor with the essence of a storm. The first time an <a href="../combat/adjacent/">adjacent</a> creature deals damage to you before the end of your next <a href="../combat/turn/">turn</a>, they take lightning damage equal to your highest <a href="../character/characteristic/">characteristic</a> score and you can <a href="../../movement/forced-movement/">push</a> them 1 square.</p>
</div></section>

<section class="pj__item"><div class="pj__item-head"><span class="dia" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="pj__item-name">Absorption</span></div>
<div class="pj__item-body">
<p>Whenever you are targeted by a magic or psionic ability that targets only one creature, you can use a free <a href="../combat/triggered-action/">triggered action</a> to cause this armor to absorb the ability after the ability's effects resolve. While the armor has an ability absorbed, you can't absorb another.</p>
<p>You can use an absorbed ability as if you knew it, making <a href="../dice/power-roll/">power rolls</a> for the ability using your choice of <a href="../character/reason/">Reason</a>, <a href="../character/intuition/">Intuition</a>, or <a href="../character/presence/">Presence</a>. You don't need to spend any <a href="../resource/heroic-resource/">Heroic Resource</a> to activate the ability. Once you use the ability, the armor loses it, and you can absorb another.</p>
</div></section>
<section class="pj__item"><div class="pj__item-head"><span class="dia" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="pj__item-name"><a href="../../damage/damage-immunity/">Damage Immunity</a> II</span></div>
<div class="pj__item-body">
<p>The <a href="../damage/damage-immunity/">damage immunity</a> conveyed by the armor increases to 10.</p>
</div></section>
<section class="pj__item"><div class="pj__item-head"><span class="dia" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="pj__item-name">Dragon Soul</span></div>
<div class="pj__item-body">
<p>When another creature causes you to be <a href="../health/winded/">winded</a> or <a href="../health/dying/">dying</a>, you can use a free <a href="../combat/triggered-action/">triggered action</a> to cause the soul of a dragon to emerge from this armor and hurtle toward the creature. Make the following <a href="../dice/power-roll/">power roll</a> against the creature.</p>
<p><strong><a href="../dice/power-roll/">Power Roll</a> + Your Highest <a href="../character/characteristic/">Characteristic</a> Score:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>≤11:</strong> 8 damage; <a href="../../movement/forced-movement/">push</a> 3</li>
<li><strong>12-16:</strong> 12 damage; <a href="../../movement/forced-movement/">push</a> 4</li>
<li><strong>17+:</strong> 15 damage; <a href="../../movement/forced-movement/">push</a> 5</li>
</ul>
</div></section>
<section class="pj__item"><div class="pj__item-head"><span class="dia" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="pj__item-name">Levitating</span></div>
<div class="pj__item-body">
<p>On your <a href="../combat/turn/">turn</a>, you can treat up to 5 consecutive squares of movement as <a href="../../movement/fly/">flying</a> movement. If you are still in midair at the end of your <a href="../combat/turn/">turn</a>, you <a href="../../condition/prone/">fall prone</a>.</p>
</div></section>
<section class="pj__item"><div class="pj__item-head"><span class="dia" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="pj__item-name">Magic Resistance II</span></div>
<div class="pj__item-body">
<p>Your <a href="../character/characteristic/">characteristic</a> scores are treated as 2 higher (to a maximum of 3) for the purpose of resisting the <a href="../character/potency/">potencies</a> of magic abilities. This benefit replaces Magic Resistance I.</p>
</div></section>
<section class="pj__item"><div class="pj__item-head"><span class="dia" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="pj__item-name">Phasing II</span></div>
<div class="pj__item-body">
<p>When you use the armor's Phasing I <a href="enhancement/">enhancement</a>, you can move through 3 squares of solid matter per <a href="../combat/turn/">turn</a>.</p>
</div></section>
<section class="pj__item"><div class="pj__item-head"><span class="dia" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="pj__item-name">Psionic Resistance II</span></div>
<div class="pj__item-body">
<p>Your <a href="../character/characteristic/">characteristic</a> scores are treated as 2 higher (to a maximum of 3) for the purpose of resisting the <a href="../character/potency/">potencies</a> of psionic abilities. This benefit replaces Psionic Resistance I.</p>
</div></section>
<section class="pj__item"><div class="pj__item-head"><span class="dia" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="pj__item-name">Reactive</span></div>
<div class="pj__item-body">
<p>Whenever you take damage, you have <a href="../damage/damage-immunity/">damage immunity</a> 2 until the end of your next <a href="../combat/turn/">turn</a> after the triggering damage is resolved.</p>
</div></section>
<section class="pj__item"><div class="pj__item-head"><span class="dia" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="pj__item-name">Second Wind</span></div>
<div class="pj__item-body">
<p>Whenever you become <a href="../health/winded/">winded</a>, you can use a free <a href="../combat/triggered-action/">triggered action</a> to spend a <a href="../health/recoveries/">Recovery</a>.</p>
</div></section>
<section class="pj__item"><div class="pj__item-head"><span class="dia" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="pj__item-name">Shattering</span></div>
<div class="pj__item-body">
<p>Whenever an enemy scores a <a href="../combat/critical-hit/">critical hit</a> against you, they take 10 sonic damage.</p>
</div></section>
<section class="pj__item"><div class="pj__item-head"><span class="dia" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="pj__item-name">Tempest II</span></div>
<div class="pj__item-body">
<p>When you use the armor's Tempest I <a href="enhancement/">enhancement</a>, the affected creature takes 8 lightning damage and you <a href="../../movement/forced-movement/">push</a> them up to 3 squares.</p>
</div></section>

<div class="pj__item-body">
<p>Your movement doesn't provoke <a href="../combat/opportunity-attack/">opportunity attacks</a>, and you can move through the space of any enemy as if they were an ally. You can't end your <a href="../combat/turn/">turn</a> in an enemy's space.</p>

</div></section>
<section class="pj__item"><div class="pj__item-head"><span class="dia" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="pj__item-name">Temporal Flux</span></div>
<div class="pj__item-body">
<p>Whenever you move out of a square, you can choose to leave an imprint behind that lasts until the end of the encounter, until your imprint takes 20 or more damage, or until you create a new imprint. The square is occupied by your imprint, and you can share that space with it.</p>
<p>On your <a href="../combat/turn/">turn</a>, you can <a href="../../movement/teleport/">teleport</a> to the imprint's space as a <a href="../combat/free-maneuver/">free maneuver</a>. When you are targeted by an ability, you can use a free <a href="../combat/triggered-action/">triggered action</a> to <a href="../../movement/teleport/">teleport</a> to your imprint, and the <a href="../dice/power-roll/">power roll</a> for the ability is an automatic tier 1 result.</p>
</div></section>
<section class="pj__item"><div class="pj__item-head"><span class="dia" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="pj__item-name">Unbending</span></div>
<div class="pj__item-body">
<p>You can't be subjected to <a href="../../movement/forced-movement/">forced movement</a> unless you choose to be. Effects that ignore <a href="../character/stability/">Stability</a> also ignore this <a href="enhancement/">enhancement</a>.</p>
