# Crafting Projects

---

<p>Crafting projects enable heroes to create vehicles, <a href="../general/supernatural/">supernatural</a> treasures, and more. When you start a crafting project, other creatures can also work on the project, using their <a href="../resource/respite/">respite</a> activity to contribute a <a href="project-roll/">project roll</a> in order to get the work done faster.</p>
<p>Unless a project has an event table of its own, the Director uses the Crafting and Research Events table for crafting <a href="project-event/">project events</a>.</p>
<h2 id="build-airship" data-scc="mcdm.heroes.v1/project/build-airship">Build Airship</h2>
<p><strong><a href="item-prerequisite/">Item Prerequisite</a>:</strong> Wind Crystal of Quintessence</p>
<p><strong><a href="project-source/">Project Source</a>:</strong> Texts or lore in Low Rhyvian</p>
<p><strong><a href="project-roll/">Project Roll</a> <a href="../character/characteristic/">Characteristic</a>:</strong> <a href="../character/might/">Might</a>, <a href="../character/reason/">Reason</a>, or <a href="../character/presence/">Presence</a></p>
<p><strong>Project Goal:</strong> 3,000</p>
<p>When you start this project, you hire a crew of carpenters, mages, and shipwrights who work in the area where the project is undertaken, with these artisans building the ship for you. You can make a <a href="project-roll/">project roll</a> whenever you are overseeing the project, which you might be able to do remotely through the use of magic or psionics.</p>
<p>When the project is completed, you have an airship. You and any creatures you designate can operate the ship by touching the ship's wheel. During combat, a creature touching the wheel can use a maneuver to make the ship move up to 10 squares. The ship can be moved only once per round. Out of combat, the ship has a <a href="../character/speed/">speed</a> of 130 miles per day.</p>
<p>An airship is an object (giving it <a href="../damage/damage-immunity/">damage immunity</a> all to poison and psychic damage) and can take 200 damage before it is destroyed. If the damage the ship takes is not too severe (as the Director determines), as long as you have access to materials that can repair the ship, you can restore it back to its original <a href="../combat/condition/">condition</a> as a <a href="../resource/respite/">respite</a> activity.</p>
<h2 id="build-or-repair-road" data-scc="mcdm.heroes.v1/project/build-or-repair-road">Build or Repair Road</h2>
<p><strong><a href="item-prerequisite/">Item Prerequisite</a>:</strong> Three writs of approval, from an engineers' guild, a masons' guild, and a guards' guild</p>
<p><strong><a href="project-source/">Project Source</a>:</strong> Texts or lore in Caelian</p>
<p><strong><a href="project-roll/">Project Roll</a> <a href="../character/characteristic/">Characteristic</a>:</strong> <a href="../character/might/">Might</a>, <a href="../character/reason/">Reason</a>, or <a href="../character/presence/">Presence</a></p>
<p><strong>Project Goal:</strong> Varies</p>
<p>When you start this project, you hire a crew of masons, engineers, and guards who start work at the location where the project begins and build or repair the road for you. You can make a <a href="project-roll/">project roll</a> whenever you are overseeing the project, whether you do so in person or remotely through the use of magic or psionics.</p>
<p>The number of <a href="project-points/">project points</a> required to complete work on the road equals 10 x the road's length in miles. The goal is cut in half if you are repairing an existing road, or if someone else starts work on a second road project that connects to your project.</p>
<p>When you complete the project, you earn <a href="../resource/renown/">Renown</a> among people in the area where the road is built, depending on the length of the road.</p>
<p>Time spent traveling between locations on the road is cut in half. Access to resources and knowledge is improved in locations along the road, giving you and your allies an <a href="../dice/edge/">edge</a> on <a href="project-roll/">project rolls</a> to discover lore while you are in those areas.</p>

<h2 id="craft-teleportation-platform" data-scc="mcdm.heroes.v1/project/craft-teleportation-platform">Craft Teleportation Platform</h2>
<p><strong><a href="item-prerequisite/">Item Prerequisite</a>:</strong> One spatial navigator</p>
<p><strong><a href="project-source/">Project Source</a>:</strong> Texts or lore in Voll</p>
<p><strong><a href="project-roll/">Project Roll</a> <a href="../character/characteristic/">Characteristic</a>:</strong> <a href="../character/reason/">Reason</a></p>
<p><strong>Project Goal:</strong> 1,500</p>
<p>When you complete this project, you end up with a 3-square-by-3 square <a href="../../movement/teleport/">teleport</a>ation platform activated by a <a href="../general/supernatural/">supernatural</a> password. The platform is permanently affixed to the ground in the location where you create it. Any creature with the password can use the <a href="../../movement/teleport/">teleport</a>ation platform to instantly <a href="../../movement/teleport/">teleport</a> any creatures or objects on the platform to a location they know, including another <a href="../../movement/teleport/">teleport</a>ation platform whose <a href="../general/supernatural/">supernatural</a> password they know. You and any creature you designate upon completion of the activity can change the password as a <a href="../resource/respite/">respite</a> activity.</p>
<p>Each time the <a href="../../movement/teleport/">teleport</a>ation platform is used, the chance of it malfunctioning and <a href="../../movement/teleport/">teleporting</a> creatures or objects on it to a random platform increases by 1 percent (to a maximum of 50 percent). You can restore the platform back to its original <a href="../combat/condition/">condition</a> and reset this chance of malfunction as a <a href="../resource/respite/">respite</a> activity.</p>
<h2 id="craft-treasure" data-scc="mcdm.heroes.v1/project/craft-treasure">Craft Treasure</h2>
<p>You can craft the items found in Treasures in Chapter 13: <a href="../../../Read/heroes/rewards/">Rewards</a>. Each treasure notes its <a href="item-prerequisite/">item prerequisite</a>, <a href="project-source/">project source</a>, <a href="project-roll/">project roll</a> <a href="../character/characteristic/">characteristic</a>, and project goal.</p>
<h2 id="find-a-cure" data-scc="mcdm.heroes.v1/project/find-a-cure">Find a Cure</h2>
<p><strong><a href="item-prerequisite/">Item Prerequisite</a>:</strong> Varies (see description)</p>
<p><strong><a href="project-source/">Project Source</a>:</strong> None</p>
<p><strong><a href="project-roll/">Project Roll</a> <a href="../character/characteristic/">Characteristic</a>:</strong> <a href="../character/reason/">Reason</a> or <a href="../character/intuition/">Intuition</a></p>
<p><strong>Project Goal:</strong> Varies</p>
<p>You research a cure for a disease, curse, or <a href="../general/supernatural/">supernatural</a> affliction whose text states that it can be ended by the Find a Cure downtime project. If the affliction was caused by the innate feature of a creature, you need the remains of that creature or another creature of the same kind as an <a href="item-prerequisite/">item prerequisite</a> for crafting the cure. For example, you can research the cure for the affliction of a werewolf's Accursed Bite by studying the corpse of any werewolf, not just the one who did the biting.</p>
<p>The number of <a href="project-points/">project points</a> it takes to complete this project is equal to 50 times the level of the creature who caused the affliction. If the affliction was created by an environmental or <a href="../general/supernatural/">supernatural</a> effect, that effect notes the <a href="item-prerequisite/">item prerequisite</a> and <a href="project-points/">project points</a> required to complete this project.</p>
<p>When you complete this project, you craft one dose of an alchemical cure for the affliction. Once the project is completed, you can craft subsequent doses for the same affliction for half the number of <a href="project-points/">project points</a> required for the initial project.</p>
<h2 id="imbue-treasure" data-scc="mcdm.heroes.v1/project/imbue-treasure">Imbue Treasure</h2>
<p>You can create <a href="../treasure/leveled-treasure/">leveled treasures</a> by imbuing items with magic or psionic power. You must have a mundane version of the item you plan to imbue, such as a sword or shield, when you start this project.</p>
<p>You can imbue any item three times. The first time you imbue it, you pick a 1st-level <a href="../treasure/enhancement/">enhancement</a>. The second time you imbue it, you pick a 5th-level <a href="../treasure/enhancement/">enhancement</a>. The third time you imbue it, you pick a 9th-level <a href="../treasure/enhancement/">enhancement</a>. A hero using armor or a weapon can't benefit from an <a href="../treasure/enhancement/">enhancement</a> unless they are using a kit that includes the armor or weapon type, and their level matches the <a href="../treasure/enhancement/">enhancement</a>'s level.</p>
<p>If an <a href="../treasure/enhancement/">enhancement</a> has a Roman numeral greater than I after it, such as the Phasing III <a href="../treasure/enhancement/">enhancement</a>, that <a href="../treasure/enhancement/">enhancement</a> can't be applied unless the item has all previous versions of the <a href="../treasure/enhancement/">enhancement</a>. This means you can't imbue armor with the Phasing III <a href="../treasure/enhancement/">enhancement</a> unless it has the Phasing I and Phasing II <a href="../treasure/enhancement/">enhancements</a> first. The effects of numbered <a href="../treasure/enhancement/">enhancements</a> are cumulative unless otherwise noted.</p>
<p><a href="../treasure/enhancement/">Enhancements</a> are broken out by item type (armor, <a href="../treasure/implement/">implement</a>, or weapon) and level. Each item type and level features a table showing the name, <a href="item-prerequisite/">item prerequisite</a>, and <a href="project-source/">project source</a> language of each <a href="../treasure/enhancement/">enhancement</a>. Imbued items take the Magic or Psionic keyword (your choice) and are part of any kit categories appropriate to the type of item (light armor, medium weapon, and so forth).</p>
<p>Imbuing treasure with <a href="../general/supernatural/">supernatural</a> power as a downtime project typically means you undertake that project to make use of the treasure yourself. As such, all the features of imbuing refer to &quot;you&quot; as the user of the item. However, imbued items can be created for other creatures, or created by other creatures and found as treasure.</p>
<h3 id="imbue-armor" data-scc="mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.treasure/enhancement">Imbue Armor</h3>
<p>Armor imbued with an enhancement grants you special benefits while it is worn. Additionally, when your armor receives its 1st-level enhancement, it also grants a +6 <a href="../dice/bonuses-and-penalties/">bonus</a> to <a href="../health/stamina/">Stamina</a>. A 5th-level enhancement increases the <a href="../health/stamina/">Stamina</a> <a href="../dice/bonuses-and-penalties/">bonus</a> to +12, and a 9th-level enhancement increases it to +21.</p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Clothing as Armor Treasures</strong></p>
<p>A hero who has a kit that provides no armor, such as the <a href="../../kit/martial-artist/">Martial Artist</a> or <a href="../../kit/sniper/">Sniper</a> kit, can create and make use of leveled armor treasures that imbue mundane clothing with <a href="../general/supernatural/">supernatural</a> power. At the Director's discretion, heroes who don't use kits, such as <a href="../../class/elementalist/">elementalists</a> and <a href="../../class/null/">nulls</a>, can also benefit from such treasures.</p>
</blockquote>

<div class="pj__item-body">

<h3 id="imbue-weapon">Imbue Weapon</h3>
<p>A weapon imbued with an <a href="../treasure/enhancement/">enhancement</a> grants you special benefits while it is wielded. Additionally, when a weapon receives its 1st-level <a href="../treasure/enhancement/">enhancement</a>, it grants your weapon abilities that deal <a href="../damage/rolled-damage/">rolled damage</a> a +1 damage <a href="../dice/bonuses-and-penalties/">bonus</a>. A 5th-level <a href="../treasure/enhancement/">enhancement</a> increases the damage <a href="../dice/bonuses-and-penalties/">bonus</a> to +2, and a 9th-level <a href="../treasure/enhancement/">enhancement</a> increases it to +3.</p>
<h4 id="unarmed-strike-treasures">Unarmed Strike Treasures</h4>
<p>A hero who has a kit that uses unarmed <a href="../combat/strike/">strikes</a>, such as the <a href="../../kit/martial-artist/">Martial Artist</a> and <a href="../../kit/pugilist/">Pugilist</a> kit, can create leveled weapon treasures that enhance their unarmed <a href="../combat/strike/">strikes</a> by imbuing hand wraps, rings, shoes, or any other item worn on the body with <a href="../general/supernatural/">supernatural</a> power. At the Director's discretion, heroes who don't use kits, such as <a href="../../class/elementalist/">elementalists</a> and <a href="../../class/null/">nulls</a>, can also benefit from such treasures.</p>
