# Suffocating

---

During combat or under similarly stressful circumstances, you can hold your breath for a number of [combat rounds](../combat/combat-round.md) equal to your [Might](../character/might.md) score (minimum 1 round). At the end of each [combat round](../combat/combat-round.md) after that, you take 1d6 damage while holding your breath.

Out of combat, you can hold your breath for a number of minutes equal to your [Might](../character/might.md) score. Being unable to breathe after that time counts as a stressful [condition](../combat/condition.md), causing you to run out of air as above.
