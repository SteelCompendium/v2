# Rolled Damage

---

Certain effects talk about rolled damage, which refers to the variable damage determined by making an [ability roll](../dice/ability-roll.md). If an ability or effect deals damage without requiring a [power roll](../dice/power-roll.md), that is not rolled damage, and effects that add to or are triggered by rolled damage don't apply.
