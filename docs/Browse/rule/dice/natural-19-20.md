# Natural 19 or 20: Success With a Reward

---

Whenever you get a natural 19 or 20 on the [power roll](power-roll.md) for a [test](../test/test.md)—a total of 19 or 20 before adding your [characteristic](../character/characteristic.md) score or other modifiers you score a critical success. This critical success automatically lets you succeed on the task with a reward, even if the [test](../test/test.md) has a medium or hard difficulty.
