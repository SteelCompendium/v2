# Patience

---

An [NPC](../general/npc.md)'s patience represents how much time and effort they're willing to devote to a negotiation. Patience is graded on a scale of 0 to 5, with each [NPC](../general/npc.md) starting a negotiation with their patience higher than 0. If an [NPC](../general/npc.md)'s patience reaches 0, the [NPC](../general/npc.md) makes a final offer and negotiation ends (see Keep Going or Stop).

Patience can decrease each time the heroes make an argument during a negotiation.

## Language and Patience

If one or more heroes negotiating with an [NPC](../general/npc.md) can communicate in the [NPC](../general/npc.md)'s native language (not including Caelian), then the [NPC](../general/npc.md)'s patience increases by 1 at the start of the negotiation (to a maximum of 5). If three or more heroes negotiating with an [NPC](../general/npc.md) can communicate in the [NPC](../general/npc.md)'s native language, the [NPC](../general/npc.md)'s patience increases by 2 (to a maximum of 5). Chapter 4: [Background](../../../Read/heroes/background.md) has information on some of the languages in the game.
