# Pitfalls

---

Pitfalls are [motivations](motivation.md) that spark ire, discomfort, shame, fear, or some other negative response in an [NPC](../general/npc.md). Using a pitfall in an argument causes an [NPC](../general/npc.md)'s [interest](interest.md) and [patience](patience.md) to wane. Each [NPC](../general/npc.md) has at least one pitfall, and many have at least two.

Pitfalls and [motivations](motivation.md) are two sides of the same concept. They're presented below as a single list, because what might be a [motivation](motivation.md) for one [NPC](../general/npc.md) is a pitfall for another. Whenever the heroes make an argument, they risk stumbling into one of an [NPC](../general/npc.md)'s pitfalls unless they do their research beforehand or read the [NPC](../general/npc.md) well.
