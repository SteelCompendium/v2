# Elite

---

Elite creatures are the functional opposite of [minions](minion.md). A creature noted as an elite is hardy and can usually stand up to two heroes of the same level. Elites also have a high [encounter value](../monster/encounter-value.md). They work well when individually supporting monsters with other modes of organization, but multiple elites can also be effective as a band on their own.
