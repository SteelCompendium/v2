# Leader

---

A leader is a powerful monster who buffs their allies and grants them additional actions. They utilize [villain actions](../monster/villain-action.md) and can stand toe-to-toe with two or more heroes of the same level by themself. Typically, only one leader appears in a battle at a time, alongside [minions](minion.md), [horde](horde.md) or [platoon](platoon.md) creatures, and [elites](elite.md). Leader creatures have no additional creature role (see Creature Roles).
