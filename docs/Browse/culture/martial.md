# Martial

---

A hero with a [martial](martial.md) upbringing was raised by warriors. These might have been the soldiers of an established army, a band of mercenaries, a guild of monster-slaying adventurers, or any other folk whose lives revolve around combat. Heroes with a [martial](martial.md) upbringing are always ready for a fight—and they know how to finish that fight.

**Skill Options:** One of the following: [Blacksmithing](../skill/crafting/blacksmithing.md) or [Fletching](../skill/crafting/fletching.md) from the [crafting skill group](../skill/crafting/index.md); [Climb](../skill/exploration/climb.md), [Endurance](../skill/exploration/endurance.md), or [Ride](../skill/exploration/ride.md) from the [exploration group](../skill/exploration/index.md); [Intimidate](../skill/interpersonal/intimidate.md) from the [interpersonal group](../skill/interpersonal/index.md); [Alertness](../skill/intrigue/alertness.md) or [Track](../skill/intrigue/track.md) from the [intrigue group](../skill/intrigue/index.md); or [Monsters](../skill/lore/monsters.md) or [Strategy](../skill/lore/strategy.md) from the [lore skill group](../skill/lore/index.md) (*Quick Build:* [Intimidate](../skill/interpersonal/intimidate.md).)
