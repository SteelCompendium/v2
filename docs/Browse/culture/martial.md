# Martial

---

A hero with a [martial](martial.md) upbringing was raised by warriors. These might have been the soldiers of an established army, a band of mercenaries, a guild of monster-slaying adventurers, or any other folk whose lives revolve around combat. Heroes with a [martial](martial.md) upbringing are always ready for a fight—and they know how to finish that fight.

**Skill Options:** One of the following: [Blacksmithing](../skill/blacksmithing.md) or [Fletching](../skill/fletching.md) from the crafting skill group; [Climb](../skill/climb.md), [Endurance](../skill/endurance.md), or [Ride](../skill/ride.md) from the exploration group; [Intimidate](../skill/intimidate.md) from the interpersonal group; [Alertness](../skill/alertness.md) or [Track](../skill/track.md) from the intrigue group; or [Monsters](../skill/monsters.md) or [Strategy](../skill/strategy.md) from the lore skill group (*Quick Build:* [Intimidate](../skill/intimidate.md).)
