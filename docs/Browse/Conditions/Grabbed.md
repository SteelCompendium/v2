##### Grabbed

A creature who is [grabbed](../../Browse/Conditions/Grabbed.md#grabbed) has speed 0, can't be force moved except by a creature, object, or effect that has them [grabbed](../../Browse/Conditions/Grabbed.md#grabbed), can't use the [Knockback](../../Browse/Abilities/Common/Maneuvers/Knockback.md#knockback) maneuver (see [Maneuvers](#page-287-0) in [Chapter 10: Combat](#page-288-0)), and takes a bane on abilities that don't target the creature, object, or effect that has them [grabbed](../../Browse/Conditions/Grabbed.md#grabbed). If a creature is [grabbed](../../Browse/Conditions/Grabbed.md#grabbed) by another creature and that creature moves, they bring the [grabbed](../../Browse/Conditions/Grabbed.md#grabbed) creature with them. If a creature's size is equal to or less than the size of a creature they have [grabbed](../../Browse/Conditions/Grabbed.md#grabbed), their speed is halved while they have that creature [grabbed](../../Browse/Conditions/Grabbed.md#grabbed).

A creature who has another creature [grabbed](../../Browse/Conditions/Grabbed.md#grabbed) can use a maneuver to move the [grabbed](../../Browse/Conditions/Grabbed.md#grabbed) creature into an unoccupied space adjacent to them.

A creature can release a creature they have [grabbed](../../Browse/Conditions/Grabbed.md#grabbed) at any time to end that condition (no action required). A [grabbed](../../Browse/Conditions/Grabbed.md#grabbed) creature can attempt to escape being [grabbed](../../Browse/Conditions/Grabbed.md#grabbed) using the [Escape Grab](../../Browse/Abilities/Common/Maneuvers/Escape Grab.md#e-capegrab) maneuver (see [Chapter 10: Combat](#page-288-0)). If a [grabbed](../../Browse/Conditions/Grabbed.md#grabbed) creature [teleports](../../Browse/Movement/Teleport.md#teleport), or if either the [grabbed](../../Browse/Conditions/Grabbed.md#grabbed) creature or the creature grabbing them is force moved so that both creatures are not adjacent to each other, that creature is no longer [grabbed](../../Browse/Conditions/Grabbed.md#grabbed).

A creature can [grab](../../Browse/Abilities/Common/Maneuvers/Grab.md#grab) only creatures of their size or smaller. If a creature's Might score is 2 or higher, they can [grab](../../Browse/Abilities/Common/Maneuvers/Grab.md#grab) any creature larger than them with a size equal to or less than their Might score.

Unless otherwise indicated, a creature can [grab](../../Browse/Abilities/Common/Maneuvers/Grab.md#grab) only one creature at a time.
