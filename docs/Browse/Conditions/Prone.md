##### Prone

While a creature is [prone](../../Browse/Conditions/Prone.md#prone), they are flat on the ground, any strike they make takes a bane, and melee abilities used against them gain an edge. A [prone](../../Browse/Conditions/Prone.md#prone) creature must [crawl](../../Browse/Movement/Crawl.md#crawl) to move along the ground, which costs 1 additional square of movement for every square crawled. A creature can't [climb](../../Browse/Movement/Climb or Swim.md#climb-or-swim), [jump](../../Browse/Movement/Jump.md#jump), [swim](../../Browse/Movement/Climb or Swim.md#climb-or-swim), or [fly](../../Browse/Movement/Fly.md#fly) while [prone](../../Browse/Conditions/Prone.md#prone). If they are [climbing](../../Browse/Movement/Climb or Swim.md#climb-or-swim), [flying](../../Browse/Movement/Fly.md#fly), or [jumping](../../Browse/Movement/Jump.md#jump) when knocked [prone](../../Browse/Conditions/Prone.md#prone), they fall.

Unless the ability or effect that imposed the [prone](../../Browse/Conditions/Prone.md#prone) condition says otherwise, a [prone](../../Browse/Conditions/Prone.md#prone) creature can stand up using the Stand Up maneuver (see [Maneuvers](#page-287-0) in [Chapter 10: Combat](#page-288-0)). A creature adjacent to a willing [prone](../../Browse/Conditions/Prone.md#prone) creature can likewise use the Stand Up maneuver to make that creature stand up.
