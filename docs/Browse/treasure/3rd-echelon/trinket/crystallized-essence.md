# Crystallized Essence

---

*A storm rages at the center of this color-shifting shard of rock.*

**Keywords:** Magic

**Item Prerequisite:** The remains of an elemental

**Project Source:** Records in Rhorvic

**Special:** The treasure must be crafted within a cave in Quintessence

**Project Roll Characteristic:** [Reason](../../../rule/character/reason.md) or [Intuition](../../../rule/character/intuition.md)

**Project Goal:** 450

**Effect:** While holding crystallized essence, the [distance](../../../rule/combat/distance.md) of your ranged magic abilities increases by 5. Any creatures you create or summon using magic have their [Stamina](../../../rule/health/stamina.md) increased by one-third of their Stamina maximum.

Additionally, you can shatter and destroy the crystallized essence as a maneuver to immediately give yourself 5 [essence](../../../feature/summoner/level-1/essence.md).
