# Grasp of the Chained Hand

---

*A gauntlet of loose chains rattles, shackled to each of the user's fingers. They clatter whenever magic surges through them.*

**Keywords:** Arms, Magic

**Item Prerequisite:** Blood-soaked chains that restrained a demon for at least 30 days

**Project Source:** Texts or lore in the Tholl

**Project Roll Characteristic:** [Reason](../../../rule/character/reason.md) or [Presence](../../../rule/character/presence.md)

**Project Goal:** 300

**Effect:** While wearing this gauntlet, your ranged [free strikes](../../../feature/common/main-actions/free-strike.md) have the Magic keyword and if the target has P < average, they are magically chained to you until the end of their next turn. While chained, the target can't move farther away from you, and you can use a maneuver to either vertically [pull](../../../movement/forced-movement.md) the target up to 3 squares or [shift](../../../movement/shifting.md) yourself up to 3 squares through the air toward the target.

**Special:** If you are a [summoner](../../../class/summoner.md), you regain the use of your standard ranged [free strike](../../../feature/common/main-actions/free-strike.md) while wearing this gauntlet.
