# Warbanner of Wrath

---

*A large banner billows from this tall flagpole. Its heraldry depicts a dragon breathing fire with outstretched wings covered in holes and tears.*

**Keywords:** Magic

**Item Prerequisite:** A king's deathbed veil and a solid brass flagpole

**Project Source:** Texts or lore in Caelian

**Project Roll Characteristic:** [Reason](../../../rule/character/reason.md) or [Intuition](../../../rule/character/intuition.md)

**Project Goal:** 600

**Effect:** You can choose to regain 1 [Recovery](../../../rule/health/recoveries.md) at the end of every combat encounter while holding the banner.

Once per [turn](../../../rule/combat/turn.md) when you or an ally within 10 squares of you makes a melee [strike](../../../rule/combat/strike.md), the strike can deal extra [damage](../../../rule/damage/damage.md) based on the number of [Recoveries](../../../rule/health/recoveries.md) the striker has left. The strike deals +1 [damage](../../../rule/damage/damage.md) if the striker has more than half their [Recoveries](../../../rule/health/recoveries.md) remaining, +2 [damage](../../../rule/damage/damage.md) if the striker has half or fewer but still at least one of their [Recoveries](../../../rule/health/recoveries.md) remaining, and +5 [damage](../../../rule/damage/damage.md) if the striker has no Recoveries.
