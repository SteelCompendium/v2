# Mirror Token

---

*A gold-rimmed, mirror-faced coin trembles in the hand as if it were repelled by your touch.*

**Keywords:** Psionic

**[Item Prerequisite](../../../rule/downtime/item-prerequisite.md):** Three sheets of glass, sunbaked gold dust

**[Project Source](../../../rule/downtime/project-source.md):** Texts or lore in Variac

**[Project Roll](../../../rule/downtime/project-roll.md) [Characteristic](../../../rule/character/characteristic.md):** [Reason](../../../rule/character/reason.md) or [Intuition](../../../rule/character/intuition.md)

**Project Goal:** 45

**Effect:** While the Mirror Token is on your person and you are targeted by a [ranged](../../../rule/combat/ranged.md) [strike](../../../rule/combat/strike.md), you can use a [triggered action](../../../rule/combat/triggered-action.md) to crush the token and ignore the [strike](../../../rule/combat/strike.md). Half the damage you would have taken and any effects of the triggering strike are imposed on the creature making the [strike](../../../rule/combat/strike.md).
