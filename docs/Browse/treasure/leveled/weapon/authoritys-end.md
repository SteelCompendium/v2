# Authority's End

---

*This long, sinuous chain is composed entirely of broken links held together by unseen power.*

**Keywords:** Psionic, Whip

**[Item Prerequisite](../../../rule/downtime/item-prerequisite.md):** A lash used to punish a mutineer

**[Project Source](../../../rule/downtime/project-source.md):** Texts or lore in Khelt

**[Project Roll](../../../rule/downtime/project-roll.md) [Characteristic](../../../rule/character/characteristic.md):** [Reason](../../../rule/character/reason.md), [Intuition](../../../rule/character/intuition.md), or [Presence](../../../rule/character/presence.md)

**Project Goal:** 450

**1st Level:** Any weapon ability that deals [rolled damage](../../../rule/damage/rolled-damage.md) using this weapon gains a +1 damage [bonus](../../../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md). Whenever you damage a creature with the weapon, you can immediately use a maneuver to end one effect imposed by that creature on you or another creature within 5 squares of you.

**5th Level:** The weapon's damage [bonus](../../../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md) increases to +2. Additionally, you and each ally within 2 squares of you gains a +1 [bonus](../../../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md) to [saving throws](../../../rule/general/saving-throw.md).

**9th Level:** The weapon's damage [bonus](../../../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md) increases to +3. Additionally, you no longer need to use a maneuver to end one effect when you damage a creature with the weapon. The weapon also refuses to vie for control of your psyche, and no longer counts against the limit of [leveled treasures](../../../rule/treasure/leveled-treasure.md) you can carry safely.
