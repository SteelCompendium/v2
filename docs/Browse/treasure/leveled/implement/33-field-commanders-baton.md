# 33 Field Commanders Baton

---

*This long, ornate rod with a silver bulb head is braided with 33 green cords. A new cord starts to grow while you wield the baton.*

**Keywords:** Implement, Magic

**Item Prerequisite:** A silver ingot and a written strategy from 33 warleaders

**Project Source:** Texts or lore in Caelian

**Project Roll Characteristic:** [Reason](../../../rule/character/reason.md), [Intuition](../../../rule/character/intuition.md), or [Presence](../../../rule/character/presence.md)

**Project Goal:** 450

**Special:** If you are a [tactician](../../../class/tactician.md), you can wield this [implement](../../../rule/treasure/implement.md) as if it had the Light Weapon keyword. Replace any reference to magic or psionic abilities with weapon abilities.

**1st Level:** While you wield this baton, you are imbued with the experience of the commanders who wielded it before you. Your magic or psionic abilities that deal [rolled damage](../../../rule/damage/rolled-damage.md) gain a +1 [damage](../../../rule/damage/damage.md) bonus. On your [turn](../../../rule/combat/turn.md), whenever you or an ally deal [damage](../../../rule/damage/damage.md) to a creature within 5 squares of you, you can immediately use your maneuver to [slide](../../../movement/forced-movement.md) the creature up to 2 squares.

**5th Level:** The baton's [damage](../../../rule/damage/damage.md) bonus increases to +2. When you [slide](../../../movement/forced-movement.md) a creature using this ability and they end this slide [adjacent](../../../rule/combat/adjacent.md) to one of your allies, that ally can use the [Grab](../../../feature/common/maneuvers/grab.md) or [Knockback](../../../feature/common/maneuvers/knockback.md) maneuver against the creature as a free [triggered action](../../../rule/combat/triggered-action.md).

**9th Level:** The baton's [damage](../../../rule/damage/damage.md) bonus increases to +3. On your [turn](../../../rule/combat/turn.md), whenever you or an ally deal [damage](../../../rule/damage/damage.md) to a creature within 10 squares of you, you can immediately use your maneuver to [slide](../../../movement/forced-movement.md) the creature up to 3 squares, ignoring [stability](../../../rule/character/stability.md).
