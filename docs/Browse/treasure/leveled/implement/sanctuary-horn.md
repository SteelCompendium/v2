# Sanctuary Horn

---

*This spiral hunting horn is embellished with branching veins of copper across the body. The metal glows red hot as the horn is blown.*

**Keywords:** Implement, Magic

**Item Prerequisite:** The spiral shell of a fallen armory snail and whirlwinds captured from Quintessence

**Project Source:** Texts or lore in Kalliak

**Project Roll Characteristic:** [Might](../../../rule/character/might.md), [Reason](../../../rule/character/reason.md), or [Intuition](../../../rule/character/intuition.md)

**Project Goal:** 450

**1st Level:** While you wield the horn, your magic and psionic [strikes](../../../rule/combat/strike.md) deal sonic [damage](../../../rule/damage/damage.md) instead of their usual damage. Additionally, you can use a maneuver once per [turn](../../../rule/combat/turn.md) to blow the horn, allowing one ally or up to two minions you control within 5 squares of you to be recalled, instantly [teleporting](../../../movement/teleport.md) them into unoccupied spaces [adjacent](../../../rule/combat/adjacent.md) to you.

**5th Level:** While you wield the horn, the area of your [cube](../../../rule/combat/cube.md), [burst](../../../rule/combat/burst.md), and [aura](../../../rule/combat/aura.md) magic or psionic abilities increases by 1. Additionally, whenever a creature is [teleported](../../../movement/teleport.md) by this horn, they can choose to either spend a [Recovery](../../../rule/health/recoveries.md) or gain a [surge](../../../rule/resource/surge.md).

**9th Level:** The horn's area bonus increases to 2. After you blow the horn as a maneuver, you can [teleport](../../../movement/teleport.md) yourself, another ally, or up to two other minions you control within 5 squares of you into the space left behind by a recalled target, provided they fit into the space.
