# Abjurer's Bastion

---

*An ornate ring is set with a large diamond that swirls with blue light, and whose inner surface is etched with protective runes.*

**Keywords:** [Implement](../../../rule/treasure/implement.md), Magic

**[Item Prerequisite](../../../rule/downtime/item-prerequisite.md):** A diamond ring

**[Project Source](../../../rule/downtime/project-source.md):** Texts or lore in Caelian

**[Project Roll](../../../rule/downtime/project-roll.md) [Characteristic](../../../rule/character/characteristic.md):** [Might](../../../rule/character/might.md), [Reason](../../../rule/character/reason.md), or [Intuition](../../../rule/character/intuition.md)

**Project Goal:** 450

**1st Level:** While you wield this [implement](../../../rule/treasure/implement.md), your magic or psionic abilities that deal [rolled damage](../../../rule/damage/rolled-damage.md) gain a +1 damage [bonus](../../../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md). Additionally, whenever you deal [rolled damage](../../../rule/damage/rolled-damage.md) to a creature using a magic or psionic ability, you gain [temporary Stamina](../../../rule/health/temporary-stamina.md) equal to your highest [characteristic](../../../rule/character/characteristic.md) score.

**5th Level:** The [implement](../../../rule/treasure/implement.md)'s damage [bonus](../../../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md) increases to +2. Additionally, whenever you deal [rolled damage](../../../rule/damage/rolled-damage.md) using a magic or psionic ability, you can use a maneuver to create an immobile field of protection that is a 1 [cube](../../../rule/combat/cube.md), around yourself or around an ally within 5 squares. While in the area, you or the chosen ally has [damage immunity](../../../rule/damage/damage-immunity.md) 5. The field disappears at the start of your next [turn](../../../rule/combat/turn.md).

**9th Level:** The [implement](../../../rule/treasure/implement.md)'s damage [bonus](../../../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md) increases to +3. Whenever you deal [rolled damage](../../../rule/damage/rolled-damage.md) to a creature using a magic or psionic ability, you and each ally within 5 squares of you gains [temporary Stamina](../../../rule/health/temporary-stamina.md) equal to your highest [characteristic](../../../rule/character/characteristic.md) score. Additionally, the size of your field of protection increases to a 3 [cube](../../../rule/combat/cube.md), and it can be placed anywhere within 10 squares of you. You and each ally in the area gain its benefits.
