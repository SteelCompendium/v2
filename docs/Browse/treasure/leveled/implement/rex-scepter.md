# Rex Scepter

---

*The rod resembles a simple tree branch. It grows and braids itself into an ornate scepter in the heat of battle.*

**Keywords:** Implement, Magic

**Item Prerequisite:** A tree branch and fealty from three hundred or more sworn followers

**Project Source:** Texts or lore in Zaliac

**Project Roll Characteristic:** [Might](../../../rule/character/might.md), [Reason](../../../rule/character/reason.md), or [Presence](../../../rule/character/presence.md)

**Project Goal:** 450

**1st Level:** While you wield this scepter, your [Renown](../../../rule/resource/renown.md) is increased by 1. Additionally, you can use a main action once per [turn](../../../rule/combat/turn.md) targeting a willing ally or two minions you control within 5 squares of you to make a [free strike](../../../feature/common/main-actions/free-strike.md) on a target within the same [distance](../../../rule/combat/distance.md) of you as a free [triggered action](../../../rule/combat/triggered-action.md).

**5th Level:** The scepter's bonus to [Renown](../../../rule/resource/renown.md) increases to +2. Additionally, whenever a creature you command using the scepter makes a [free strike](../../../feature/common/main-actions/free-strike.md), they gain 1 [surge](../../../rule/resource/surge.md) that can be used immediately.

**9th Level:** The scepter's bonus to [Renown](../../../rule/resource/renown.md) increases to +4. Additionally, the scepter's main action can now be used as a maneuver.
