# Wand of the Unheard Orchestra

---

*This conductor's baton has an unassuming and inornate steel body. It increases in length when it's swung and flashes a bright light when wanded at a regular interval.*

**Keywords:** Implement, Magic

**Item Prerequisite:** An iron ingot and a singing tree's wood found in Arcadia

**Project Source:** Texts or lore in Khelt

**Project Roll Characteristic:** [Agility](../../../rule/character/agility.md), [Reason](../../../rule/character/reason.md), or [Intuition](../../../rule/character/intuition.md)

**Project Goal:** 450

**1st Level:** While you wield this wand, your magic or psionic abilities have their [distance](../../../rule/combat/distance.md) increased by 3. Additionally, you can use a maneuver once per [turn](../../../rule/combat/turn.md) targeting yourself or a willing ally within 5 squares of you to use a move action as a free [triggered action](../../../rule/combat/triggered-action.md).

**5th Level:** The wand's [distance](../../../rule/combat/distance.md) bonus increases to +5. Additionally, you and any minions you control have their [speed](../../../rule/character/speed.md) increased by 2. Whenever you or any minions you control take the [Disengage](../../../feature/common/move-actions/disengage.md) move action, they can [shift](../../../movement/shifting.md) 2 additional squares as part of that move action.

**9th Level:** The wand's [distance](../../../rule/combat/distance.md) bonus increases to +8. Additionally, the wand's maneuver can now be used as a [free maneuver](../../../rule/combat/free-maneuver.md) once during your [turn](../../../rule/combat/turn.md), targeting yourself or an ally within 10 squares of you to use a move action as a free [triggered action](../../../rule/combat/triggered-action.md).
