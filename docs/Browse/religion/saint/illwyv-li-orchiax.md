# Illwyv li Orchiax

---

**Domains:** Nature, Protection

Illwyv li Orchiax, the Moonknight, Marshall of the Gloaming, Manslayer. When [humans](../../ancestry/human.md) first arrived in [Orden](../../rule/world/orden.md)—it is said—they were welcomed by the other speaking peoples. But they were unlike the other creations in the world. While the [dwarves](../../ancestry/dwarf.md) cut rock for the ore within, [humans](../../ancestry/human.md) cut down trees simply because they were in the way. Other ancestries had their own territories and homelands, but [humans](../../ancestry/human.md) sought constant expansion into even the most inhospitable territories.

Illwyv it was who first realized the folly of treating with these creatures. A great hunter of the Quercus Court, she gathered her band of elite Helriath Harriers and made war on the [humans](../../ancestry/human.md) who would kill the wode.
