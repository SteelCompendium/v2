    Level 6

    Pain of Your Own Making

    Censor · Exorcist
    Level 6
    9 Wrath
    Free Triggered Action

    You reverse the effects from an evildoer.

    MagicRanged
    Distance
    Ranged 10
    Targets
    Self or one ally
    Trigger

    The target gains a condition or effect that is ended by a saving throw or that ends at the end of their turn.

    Effect

    The effect ends on the target and is applied to the creature who imposed the effect on them. That creature also takes damage equal to three times your Presence score.

    Censor · Oracle
    Level 6
    9 Wrath
    Maneuver

    You anticipate your foes' moves and deny them.

    AreaMagic
    Distance
    3 aura

    Intercede

    Censor · Paragon
    Level 6
    9 Wrath
    Free Triggered Action

    You take your ally's place.

    MagicRanged
    Distance
    Ranged 10
    Targets
    One ally
    Trigger

    A creature makes a strike against the target.

    Effect

    The target is unaffected by the strike and you become the target instead, even if you aren't a valid target for it. You take half the damage from the strike, and the target gains 3 surges.
